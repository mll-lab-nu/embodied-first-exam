Pick up the hammer and drive the nail into the wooden block by striking its head. Only a real swing counts: the hammer face must be coming down faster than about 0.2 m/s just before it hits the nail; a blow at that speed drives the nail about 8 mm deeper and a faster one further, up to about 2.4 cm per strike (from about 0.6 m/s; at most 7.26 cm in all). Success: the nail is at least 4 cm deep.

The hammer lies on the table at the robot's right (low y); the nail stands upright in a wooden block in front of the robot. The nail is not a free body: it only moves down when struck.
