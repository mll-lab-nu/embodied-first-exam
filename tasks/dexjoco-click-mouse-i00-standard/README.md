## Task
DexJoCo `click_mouse` (a Franka Panda arm with a 16-DoF Allegro hand) on the same frozen instance as `dexjoco-click-mouse-i00-privileged`: Put the computer mouse on the magenta mousepad and click its button. Success: the mouse is on the pad (its centre within the pad's 0.15 m radius horizontally and within 0.05 m of the pad's height), the button has been pressed while the mouse was on the pad (button joint more than 0.001 rad past its rest angle), which turns the monitor blue, and the mouse then stays on the pad for 10 consecutive control steps. A click while the mouse is off the pad does nothing.
Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with the robot's own
sensors, calibrated cameras (RGB, optional depth) and the robot's kinematics.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success check as `dexjoco-click-mouse-i00-privileged`. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the
simulator and the object poses: it sees camera images and proprioception, acts in chunks of tcp-target + hand-joint rows
(or joint targets), and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what the cameras show and recover from its own
mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle_trajectory.npz`, 560 rows, float32-exact)
through the client as `ee_pose` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded
trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference, and the episode ends with success after 560 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes
`final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live check succeeded, both replays succeed,
and they end in the same state. Oracle: success after 560 control steps.
