## Task
MuJoCo Playground `SpotGetup` (Boston Dynamics Spot) on the same frozen instance as `mujoco_playground_spot_getup-privileged`: Boston Dynamics Spot has fallen over. Make it get up and stand upright at its standing height.
It starts lying with its trunk tilted 128.4 deg (Playground's random drop, then a 0.5 s settle) and has 6 s to stand upright at its standing height. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with only
the robot's own sensors and four cameras that follow it.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success check as `mujoco_playground_spot_getup-privileged`. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and
the world state: it sees camera images and proprioception (joints, IMU, foot contacts), acts in joint position chunks,
and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on the robot's own sensors and recover from its own mistakes without
starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_replay.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle.npy`, 63 rows, float32-exact)
through the client in `hold` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded trajectory
is then exactly the privileged reference, and the episode ends with success after 63 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes
`final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live check succeeded, both replays succeed,
and they end in the same state. Oracle: success after 63 control steps.
