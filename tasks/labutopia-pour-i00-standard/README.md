## Task
LabUtopia's `pour` (Pour (LabUtopia's level-2 PourLiquid is the same task)) on the same frozen instance as `labutopia-pour-i00-privileged`: Pick up the small glass beaker, pour it out over the large glass beaker and bring it back upright. There is no liquid in the simulation: the pour is judged by where the small beaker is and how far it is turned. Success the moment the last of these is met: (1) the small beaker's centre has been more than 12 cm above where it stood; then, counted only while its centre is within about 8 cm (horizontally) of the large beaker's centre, (2) it has been turned more than 50 degrees from its orientation right after (1), (3) then turned back to within 30 degrees of that orientation, and (4) then stays more than 5 cm above where it stood for 84 control steps (1.4 s).
On the bench in front of the arm: two glass beakers: a small one, about 11 cm across and 9.5 cm tall (the one to pour), and a large one, about 15.5 cm across and 13 cm tall (the one to pour into). Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with only its proprioception and the scene's cameras.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `labutopia-pour-i00-privileged` (LabUtopia's level 1). Four conditions in a fixed order, three of them counted only while the held beaker is within about 8 cm of the other one's axis: a tilt of more than 50 degrees and back, in the hand, without losing the beaker. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and the state of the objects: it sees camera images (glassware is nearly transparent in them and absent from the depth maps) and the robot's proprioception, acts in joint target chunks, and gets one episode, so it has to find the things it handles in the images and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle.npz`, 985 rows, float32) through the client as `joint_position_velocity` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference: success after 985 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
