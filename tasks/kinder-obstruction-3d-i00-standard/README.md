# kinder-obstruction-3d-i00-standard

Standard mode (robot as a service, `eai-standard/2.1`) of `kinder-obstruction-3d-i00`: the same KinDER instance (`kinder/Obstruction3D-o4-v0`, seed
0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and
never the scene's state or the goal check. One episode, no reset.

Task: put the purple block on the thin purple pad on the wooden table; three of the four small red blocks sit on the pad and the fourth is elsewhere on the table. The task succeeds the moment the robot holds nothing, the block's underside is within 5 mm of the pad's top surface, and all four of its bottom corners lie within the pad's outline enlarged by 5 mm on every side.

## Difficulty
Geometric, kinematic (PyBullet): clear three blocks off a pad, then place a block on it; a move that would collide is refused. GPT-6 Luna (Codex, effort medium, 60 min; standard mode before protocol v1.0, as rcb-limited/1.1): privileged passed; standard failed, with 50 resets and with none.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability rests on the positive example in `oracle_evidence` (task.toml), checked by a human reviewer (docs/PROTOCOL.md §2.7).

## Verification
The sidecar records the episode, replays it in a fresh simulator when the collect hook ends the trial, and writes
`/data/final.json`; `tests/grade.py` grades that in a container of its own with no network: success = live success
(KinDER's goal check during the episode) ∧ replay success ∧ deterministic (the replay ends in the live run's state).
No oracle; human review in PR #8. `nop` scores 0.
