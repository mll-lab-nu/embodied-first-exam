## Difficulty
Privileged mode: the agent has every entity's pose and grasp and place points, VLABench's general skills (pick, place,
pull, press, …) as planning primitives, inverse kinematics, unlimited resets and self-replay. What remains hard is the
task's own reasoning (VLABench's instruction is a direct command) and making the skills or a hand-written controller
succeed on this scene. The standard twin removes the privileged state.

## Reference solution
793 control steps on this instance (`solution/oracle.npy`), replayed by `solution/solve.sh`. Source:
PR #4's first author reference: VLABench's skills driven by its expert sequence or by a strategy written by the PR #4 author (scripts/vlabench/custom_oracles.py on dev/changhe). VLABench's experts are removed from the image. Re-verified by `scripts/vlabench/freeze.sh`: the
replay ends with the task's success at its last step.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of
the instance (`tests/instance.json`); pass iff both end in the same state (`deterministic`) and the task succeeds at
the last action. Oracle: replays end in the live state (hash `0f7a3aea9581…`) and succeed. `nop`: no trajectory,
reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start (checked on every load).
