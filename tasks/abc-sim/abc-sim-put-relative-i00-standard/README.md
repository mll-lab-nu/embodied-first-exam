## Task
ABC Sim `put_relative` (two I2RT YAM arms (6 revolute joints and a parallel two-finger gripper each) side by side at the near edge of a table, facing +x (the left arm is the one at +y)): Set the soap dispenser down a short way to the right of the boxed food, as the arms face the table.
A few household objects stand on the table. The robot is a service; the episode it runs is graded.

## Difficulty
First-pass rating: medium. Grounding a spatial directive (which object, which reference, which direction as the arms face the table) in camera images, then one grasp and a placement. The action is joint-level (two 6-joint arms and two grippers; no motion primitives), and the goal has to hold, so passing through it does not count.

## Reference solution
The privileged twin's reference (the commanded actions of one of upstream's validation demonstrations of this task): `solution/oracle.npy`, 237 rows, streamed as `joint_position` chunks through the client (`solution/oracle_stream.py`); the service executes exactly those rows and ends at the same state as the privileged replay.

## Verification
The live episode's verdict (PROTOCOL H.4): the sim sidecar judges the episode it ran by the task's rule (the 1.02 s hold included) and the collect hook (`/opt/abc-tools/standard/finalize.sh`) writes it to `final.json`; the verifier (the shared `v2_1/grade.py`) transcribes it. Nothing is replayed to grade it; the video is rendered from the trajectory afterwards. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
