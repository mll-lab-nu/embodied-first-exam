## Task
A wall stands in front of the hovering aerial manipulator, with a horizontal slider mounted on its front face: a straight rail about 1 m long and a red knob that slides along it. The knob starts at the rail's left end (as seen from the robot facing the wall) and must be pushed along the rail until it is within 5 cm of the right end of its travel, i.e. at least 0.95 m along its 1 m travel. AM-Bench environment `PushSlider-Am-FAHexa-Abs-PID-Direct-v0` (the fully actuated hexarotor with the 4-joint arm, AM-Bench's PID
controller), its seeded reset with seed 0. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of AM-Bench's absolute
end-effector pose actions at 120 Hz (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`), graded by AM-Bench's own success check within the
task's episode length of 26 s.

## Difficulty
The knob has to be driven 0.95 m, almost the whole length of the rail, in one direction against joint friction and damping, and the fixture's place on the wall changes per instance. Privileged mode gives the full simulator state, unlimited resets and self-replay; the tooling offers only the
environment's own action, its state and its cameras.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_actions.npy` (run by `solution/oracle.py` through the agent's own tooling): AM-Bench's scripted expert
for this task, run on this instance (it reads the simulator's state in closed loop) and recorded, followed by 2 s of
holding its last command (a replay may succeed a step later than the live run; rows after success never run). The image
has no expert (`images/ambench/Dockerfile` removes them), and the agent's network reaches only the
model APIs and the package registries.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox: one replay in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the
instance (`tests/instance.json`; the rebuilt start is checked against its hash); pass iff AM-Bench's success check holds
in it (with one replay nothing is compared: `determinism_checked` 0; tasks/ambench/README.md, Determinism). `/logs/verifier/replay.mp4` (both onboard cameras) is filmed after the verdict
by the `film` CLI, the same rows drawn by the render worker, an illustration only. Oracle: success at control step
1353 (11.275 s simulated) in both fresh-process replays, which end in the frozen end state (hash
`01a85d356fead490`; a rare race can change the last bits of one, never measured to change the outcome). `nop`: no
trajectory, reward 0.
