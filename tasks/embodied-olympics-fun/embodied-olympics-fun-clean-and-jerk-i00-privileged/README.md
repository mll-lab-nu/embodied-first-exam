## Task
Clean and jerk the barbell: lift it from its blocks to your shoulders, then to locked arms overhead, and hold it there motionless until the down signal. (Embodied Olympics `clean-and-jerk`: one Unitree H1-2 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
The bar is light, but the hands hold it only by their fingers' friction. Turning it over from the hang to the shoulders, slowly, asks about half of what the elbow and shoulder motors give; too fast, and the bar slides out of the hands. Nothing balances the robot under a load, and no built-in action bends down to the bar or straightens the knees.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle.py` drives the robot through the task's own tooling, closed loop on the privileged state, and writes the trajectory (`solution/solve.sh`). Reference (real, lowcmd rows, a quasi-static whole-body plan) for a block clean & jerk: the 5 kg technique bar on two 0.35 m lifting blocks (axis 0.375 m above the platform; declared: on 0.20 m blocks the squat would need the hips flexed past where the hip links meet the pelvis, and 52 squats tried all fell onto a thigh or knocked the bar). On 0.20 m blocks the planner's bottom poses put the hips 15 to 46 mm into the pelvis, or the thumbs into the knees. Heavier bars: with the women's 15 kg bar every path of the clean's turnover passes a pose with the bar level with the elbow joint, where the best arm pose found (with a gripper whose grip was 0.11 m further from the wrist) needs 1.08 to 1.17 times the elbow and shoulder motors' torque (0.54 with 5 kg); 10 kg (the bar on two 2.5 kg technique discs) topples the robot backwards at the turnover. ## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in one fresh process (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success. The rules: The barbell is an aluminium technique bar (5.0 kg, 1.835 m long; the grip section between the inner collars is 1.31 m long and 0.025 m thick; the sleeves are 0.2325 m long and 0.05 m thick and revolve on the bar), without discs or collars: 5.0 kg. It rests by its sleeves on two lifting blocks (0.35 m high) standing on the competition platform (4.0 m by 4.0 m, 0.1 m high), on which you stand. Your hands are five-finger hands, open at the start; a hand holds the bar while the bar's grip section touches that hand's fingers (a break of up to 0.05 s is not letting go); they hold it by contact and friction only, so it can slip, turn in the hands and drop. Lift-off: the bar may leave its blocks only in both hands: you fail the moment the bar's axis is more than 0.03 m above where it lies at rest while fewer than two hands hold it. The attempt: once the bar's centre has been 0.48 m above the platform (knee height) the lift must be finished: from then on you fail if either hand lets go or if any part of the barbell touches the platform or a block before the down signal. The clean: the bar must first rest at your shoulders: its axis within 0.15 m of both shoulder joints, both hands on it, both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight, both feet on the platform and on one line, the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar (your trunk upright: the cosine of its tilt at least 0.94; the bar's centre no more than 0.25 m, horizontally, from the point midway between your shoulder joints; the bar, seen from above, within 20.0 degrees of the line through them), with the attempt begun (the bar has been above knee height and touches nothing but you), all of it without a break for 0.5 s. You fail if the bar's axis is more than 0.25 m above the centre of your head before that (a lift from the platform to overhead in one movement is a snatch). The jerk and the finish: after the clean, bring the bar overhead. The final position is: the bar's axis at least 0.25 m above the centre of your head, both hands on it, both elbows within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle between the upper arm's and the forearm's long axes), both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle hip, knee, ankle), both feet on the platform and on one line (their centres no more than 0.05 m apart along the horizontal direction square to the bar; under a bar tilted more than 60.0 degrees from level the feet never count as on one line), the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar as in the clean (trunk upright, the bar's centre over the middle of your shoulders, the bar along your shoulder line). Once it is held for 2.0 s without a break the down signal is given: that is success, and the attempt ends. At any time you fail if a part of you other than your feet touches the platform, if any part of you touches a block, if any part of you or of the barbell touches the floor beside the platform, or after 60 s. There is no other fall rule: you may squat as low as you like, and the bar may touch your body.
