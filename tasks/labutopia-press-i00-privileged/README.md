## Task
LabUtopia's `press` (Press), with a Franka Panda arm (7 joints) with its two-finger gripper, fixed to a laboratory bench: Press the red button on the instrument's front panel and keep it pressed. Success, at the first control step at which this has held for 60 consecutive control steps (1 s): the button's cap (`/World/target_button/button`) is beyond x = 0.405 m. It starts at x = 0.40 m and is pushed in along +x.
In front of the arm stands an instrument with a vertical front panel that carries three square buttons in a row, each 8 cm across: one red, two dark grey. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint targets (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 18)`, one row per 1/60 s control step: 9 position targets, then 9 velocity targets).

## Difficulty
LabUtopia's level 1. The target is one of three identical buttons told apart by colour only, on a vertical panel at arm's length; it has to be held in for a full second. The agent has full access to the simulator and to LabUtopia's scene and task code, but not to its scripted controller or its motion primitives: it has to build the motion itself from joint targets at 60 Hz.

## Reference solution
LabUtopia's scripted controller (press, config level1_press @ 8df7278), seed 0: the joint targets it sends, one row per control step (float32, as PhysX holds them), cut at the first step at which the rule is met: `solution/oracle.npz`, 328 rows, success after 328 control steps. Replayed and saved by `solution/solve.sh`; re-checked by `scripts/labutopia/make_facts.py --check` (two fresh processes, same final state).

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the rebuilt placements are checked against its recorded hash); pass iff both episodes succeed (the first control step at which the rule is met ends the episode) in the same state (`deterministic`). `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
