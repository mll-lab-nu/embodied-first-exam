## Difficulty
Full privileged state, unlimited resets and self-replay; a mobile manipulator in one of RoboCasa365's large,
cluttered kitchens, long-horizon contact-rich manipulation with a 20 Hz controller. The standard twin removes the
privileged state.

## Reference solution
The demonstration's own actions (joint: 85 actions), executed from
the frozen scene by the robot's own controller: 85 actions in `solution/oracle.npz`. The
demonstration is a reference solution: it is not in the image and the agent cannot reach it.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of
the instance; `success = deterministic ∧ success on the final state ∧ not solved at the start`. Reference: two
fresh-process replays end in the live run's state (hash `864e2e171d83…`) and succeed. `nop`: no
trajectory, reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start.
