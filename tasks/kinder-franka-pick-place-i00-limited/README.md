# kinder-franka-pick-place-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-franka-pick-place-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/FrankaPickPlace3D-o1-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- a wrist
camera and joint encoders -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: get the small dark-red cube on the desk into a rectangle on the desk top that is not marked: x from 0.299 to 0.498 m and y from 0.103 to 0.252 m in the arm's base frame (`spec()["base_frame"]`). The task succeeds the moment the cube's centre is inside that rectangle and at most 20 cm above the desk top.
