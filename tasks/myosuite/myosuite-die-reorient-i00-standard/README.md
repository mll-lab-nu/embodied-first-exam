## Task
MyoSuite `myoChallengeDieReorientP1-v0` (MyoHand, 39 muscles) on the same frozen instance as `myosuite-die-reorient-i00-privileged`: Rotate the die in the hand to the target pose: its position within 0.025 m of the target and the norm of its Euler-angle error below 0.262 rad, for 100 consecutive control steps (1.0 s). Failure (the episode ends): the die more than 0.2 m from the target (dropped).
A die rests in the palm of the upturned hand; a translucent target die shows the goal orientation. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the body through a client, with only its own proprioception and the scene's cameras, and 10 resets (only the last episode is graded).

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `myosuite-die-reorient-i00-privileged` (first-pass rating: hard). In standard mode the agent loses free resets, the simulator and the object state: it sees camera images and the limb's proprioception, acts in actuator-command chunks, and gets 10 resets, so it has to close the loop on what it sees and learn the body from a few real attempts.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: the privileged twin's positive example (Claude Code + Claude Opus 5.5 (run `claude_code-claude_opus_5_5-default`, 2026-09-28), trial `myosuite-die-reorient-i00__EtyJ3X7`, 225 rows) rounded to float32, as the client sends it, and streamed through the client: the service scores it 1 live and in both replays, in two fresh trials (tasks/myosuite/README.md); human review in PR #65.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live (the task's rule on the steps it ran: the goal held 100 control steps) and records every executed control step; it checks at the episode's start that the scene is the frozen instance (its recorded state hash; else `instance_mismatch`, an infrastructure failure). The collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service and writes that live verdict to `final.json` (`graded_by` = `live`; nothing is replayed to grade it), then renders `replay.mp4` in one pass over the recorded trajectory, which decides nothing; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded. A missing or unreadable `final.json`, one without a verdict, or a give-up after crashes not all inside the agent's calls is an infrastructure failure (`infra_error = 1`), not the agent's 0. No oracle; human review in PR #65. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
