The exemplar painting (`target.png`) was painted by the RoboPaint benchmark's robot for the benchmark (Embodied First Exam) and is
dedicated to the public domain (CC0-1.0). The paint engine is libmypaint 1.6.1 (ISC licence, github.com/mypaint/libmypaint; built
with a one-line determinism patch); the brush presets come from mypaint-brushes 2.0.2 (CC0-1.0; the release's COPYING and AUTHORS are
in /opt/mypaint/brushes/ in the image).
