Pick up the hammer and drive the nail into the wooden block by striking the nail's head with it. Only a real downward swing counts: the hammer face must be moving down faster than about 20 cm per second just before it hits the nail. Such a blow drives the nail about 1 cm deeper, and a faster blow drives it further, up to about 2.5 cm per strike. Success: the nail is at least 4 cm deep.

The hammer lies on the table at the robot's right (low y); the nail stands upright in a wooden block in front of the robot. The nail is not a free body: it only moves down when struck.
