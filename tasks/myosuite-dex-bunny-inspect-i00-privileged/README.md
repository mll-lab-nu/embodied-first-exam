## Task
MyoSuite `MyoHandStanfordbunnyInspect-v0` (MyoHand, a musculoskeletal model of a human forearm and hand, 39 muscles and 6 other actuators): Move the Stanford bunny along MyoDex's recorded inspect (lift it and turn it to look at it) motion: a reference of the object's pose over 99 control steps (1.98 s), from GRAB motion capture (TCDM / MyoDex). The episode lasts exactly those 99 steps. Success: the object's centre of mass within 0.03 m of the reference and its orientation within 0.5 rad of the reference's at at least 90% of the control steps, and at the last one. Failure (the episode ends early): MyoDex's termination, the object more than 0.25 m from the reference or from the wrist.
The Stanford bunny lies on the table; the MyoHand is at its pre-grasp pose. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of actuator commands (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 45)`, one row per 20 ms control step).

## Difficulty
First-pass rating: hard. The action is muscle-level (39 muscles, each only pulling, several crossing more than one joint, with activation delays): the agent has to build its own joint control from muscle excitations. The whole recorded motion has to be followed with the object in hand, within a few centimetres, for 99 control steps.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: no positive example yet (not attempted); the task is reported separately until one exists (PROTOCOL §7, Open 4); human review in PR #TBD.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.npz`; the rebuilt start state is checked against its recorded hash); pass iff both episodes end in success (on track over the 99-step reference) in the same state (`deterministic`). No oracle; human review in PR #TBD. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
