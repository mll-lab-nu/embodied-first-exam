## Task
Push the sled by its handles along your lane until all of it is over the line ahead.

## Success criteria
A push sled (5 kg in all, its runners sliding on turf with a friction coefficient of 0.45) stands with its front edge
on the start line, its two upright handles towards the robot; the finish line is 12.5 m beyond the start line, and
the lane runs 1 m to each side of its centre line. The handles cannot be gripped. The referee's stages, in order:
moved, line.
- Moved: the sled's rear has gone 0.1 m forward.
- Line: the whole sled, its rear included, is over the finish line. The success is called at that moment.

Fouls (each fails the attempt): any part of the robot other than a hand touches the sled; a hand touches the sled
anywhere but on a handle; any part of the sled goes outside the lane. Fall (a failure at any time): the pelvis below
0.5 m, or any part other than the feet on the ground. Time limit: 60 s (6,000 control steps).

Graded: the live attempt, judged by the referee in the simulator service as it runs; nothing is replayed. The referee's
success call is an event: once it is made at some control step, the attempt is a success. It is never shown to the
agent and does not end the attempt: the physics goes on until the agent's `/finish`, the attempt's time limit, or the
end of the agent's time (2 h), and nothing after the call undoes it. A failure the referee calls before any
success (a foul, a fall, the physics diverging) ends the attempt at once with 0. Reaching the time limit or the end of
the session without a success call scores 0. The agent has 5 resets, each starting a new attempt from the
frozen start; only the last attempt (the one after the last reset) is graded, and a success in an earlier attempt does
not count. A simulator error raised by the agent's own call ends the attempt with 0. An attempt that does not start
from the frozen instance, or a service crash outside the agent's calls, is an infrastructure failure, not a score.
