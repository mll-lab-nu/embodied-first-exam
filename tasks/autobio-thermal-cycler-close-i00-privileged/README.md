## Task
AutoBio's `thermal_cycler_close` (Close thermal cycler lid), with a UR5e arm (6 joints) with a Robotiq 2F-85 gripper, fixed to the bench: Close the lid of the thermal cycler and lock it. Success at the first control step where the lid's hinge (`/thermal_cycler_biorad_c1000:lid`) is within 0.01 rad of -1.9 rad (fully closed; it starts open, at 0) and the lid's lever (`/thermal_cycler_biorad_c1000:lid-lever`) is within 0.01 rad of -0.94 rad (the locked end of its travel; it starts at 0).
On the bench: a thermal cycler (a Bio-Rad C1000 model) with its lid open. The lid carries a lever that locks it and a force knob. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 7)`, one row per 2 ms control step).

## Difficulty
AutoBio's level: easy. Two articulated parts have to reach their end stops within 0.01 rad: the hinged lid and the lever on it that locks it. The agent has full access to the simulator and to AutoBio's task code, but not to its scripted expert: it has to build the motion itself from joint position targets at the physics rate.

## Reference solution
AutoBio's scripted expert (mani_thermal_cycler.py @ 0850421), seed 0: the position targets it sends, recorded at every physics step, clipped to the actuators' ranges, rounded to float32 (so the standard twin's client executes the same rows), cut at the first success: `solution/oracle.npz`, 5068 rows, success after 5068 control steps. Replayed and saved by `solution/solve.sh`; re-checked by `scripts/autobio/make_facts.py --check` (two fresh processes, same final state).

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the compiled model and the rebuilt start state are checked against its recorded hashes); pass iff both episodes succeed (the first control step at which AutoBio's check holds ends the episode in success) in the same state (`deterministic`). `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
