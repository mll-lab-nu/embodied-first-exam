## Task
Robot air hockey on a physical MuJoCo table: a seven-axis KUKA iiwa arm holding a mallet at each end, the puck sliding on the air cushion, in a continuous world slowed by a factor S; you command your mallet's target in your own half and see the table only through your fixed camera; one game to 7 goals under the USAA rules (seven-second rule, dead-puck face-offs, a safety cap with a stated last resort, a 0.35 m goal mouth); format match: you against a scripted opponent (level 1-4), format duel: two players; format `match` (seats A),
action level `low`, program play. Generated by `tools/generate.py` from
`sports/airhockey/sport.toml` (RoboOlympics task `airhockey-match-wallclock-program-standard`, exported with `--upstream`: tools/upstream.toml); do not edit by hand.

Deviations from the rulebook:

- D1 (I.1, III.1): one game to 7 points instead of a match of several games
- D2 (- (the rules have no time limit)): a safety cap of 120 s of play (frozen time does not count) or 40 rallies; then the player with more goals wins, at equal goals the scorer of the last goal, at 0:0 the player in whose half the puck spent less time, and if that is equal the player who did not have the first possession (the stated last resort: no draws)
- D3 (III.1-2): the game starts with the puck at rest in the half of the first player (FIRST_PLAYER, the coin toss) instead of a face-off; a face-off places the puck at rest at the table centre with both mallets at home instead of a referee's release
- D4 (I.10, VI.1): the seven-second rule is a foul with the rulebook's penalty, forfeiture of the puck: the opponent gets it at rest on its spot; a foul gives no point
- D5 (IV.6, I.11): a dead puck (play suspended) is added for a puck that no mallet can reach for 2 s (the robots' reach) or that touches the centre line for 7 s (I.11 has no limit); it is followed by a face-off
- D6 (I.4, I.14-15): after every goal (I.4: the player scored upon gets the puck), foul or dead puck both mallets return home and the world freezes for the reviews; the time-outs are not used
- D7 (I.8, I.13, VI.6): palming, goal-tending and topping cannot occur: only the mallets touch the puck and it stays flat on the table
- D8 (I.12, VI.9): the mallets cannot cross the centre line: the mallet workspace ends 0.05 m before it (and at 1.45 m from the robot's base), so the players cannot touch each other
- D9 (-): the players are seven-axis KUKA iiwa arms holding a mallet, commanded by a Cartesian target for the mallet (a smooth path of at most 2 m/s and 10 m/s^2, inverse kinematics and torque-limited motors), not human hands
- D10 (II.A.1 (the rules name the sanctioned tables, not their dimensions)): the goal mouth is 0.35 m wide (|y| <= 0.175) instead of the 0.25 m of the table model (AirHockeyChallenge, 1.948 x 1.038 m): the arms move the mallet at about 2 m/s, so a shot takes 0.5-1 s to arrive and a defender that follows the puck would cover a 0.25 m mouth almost completely

## Difficulty
Frozen instance (no run-time variables): seed 101 (it places the world and draws the execution scatter), opponent level 1, slowdown S = 10 (the world runs at 1/S of real speed). The RoboOlympics task `airhockey-match-wallclock-program-standard` sets these at run time; this export fixes one official configuration, in which the reference player wins.

## Reference solution
`solution/solve.sh` runs the sport's reference player (`solution/builtin_players.py`) through the core's client
(`solution/ro_client.py`): camera images and `/spec` only.

## Verification
The `sim` service (the v0.2 core, `environment/sim/core/`) records the match (`episode.jsonl`) and a fresh process
replays it (state hash); the verifier (`tests/grade.py` = `core/grading.py`, no network) writes the common reward
fields (`success`, `valid_trial`, `deterministic`, `<seat>_won`, ...) and the sport's fields (`tests/sport_grade.py`).
