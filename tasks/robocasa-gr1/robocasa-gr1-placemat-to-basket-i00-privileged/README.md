## Task
RoboCasa's GR1 tabletop `PosttrainPnPNovelFromPlacematToBasketSplitA` task with a Fourier GR1 humanoid (arms, waist and two dexterous six-command Fourier hands; fixed base): pick the croissant from the placemat and place it in the basket. Produce one replayable trajectory of the robot's native controller commands on one frozen instance; graded on the state after the last action. Generated by `scripts/robocasa-gr1/generate.py`; benchmark notes in `docs/benchmarks/robocasa-gr1.md`.

## Difficulty
Full privileged state, unlimited resets and self-replay; a humanoid's two arms and dexterous hands driven through a whole-body IK controller (absolute grip-site poses, six commands per hand), pick-and-place with containers and doors. The standard twin removes the privileged state.

## Reference solution
The demonstration's own actions (original: 146 actions), executed from the frozen scene by the robot's own controller: 146 actions in `solution/oracle.npz`. The demonstration is a reference solution: it is not in the image and the agent cannot reach it.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of the instance; `success = deterministic ∧ success on the final state ∧ not solved at the start`. Reference: two fresh-process replays end in the live run's state (hash `357ea8ff9897…`) and succeed. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start.
