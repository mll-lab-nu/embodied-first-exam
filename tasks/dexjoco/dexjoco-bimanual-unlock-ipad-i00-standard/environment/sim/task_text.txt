Enter the passcode 1-2-3 on the iPad's screen. You may pick the iPad up, but you do not have to. A digit counts as pressed when a fingertip of the right hand touches its button, and touches by the left hand do not count. Pressing any digit other than the next one in 1-2-3 ends the episode as a failure. Success: the screen is unlocked (1, 2, 3 in order) and stays unlocked for 10 consecutive control steps.

The iPad rests on a stand on the table, screen facing the robots; its keypad shows the digits.
