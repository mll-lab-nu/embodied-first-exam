# The exemplar (licence notice)

The exemplar picture (`target.png`) is a square crop of a public-domain painting on Wikimedia Commons (the task's
`[metadata] source_image` and `source_artist` name it), downscaled to the sheet by the RoboPaint benchmark (Embodied First
Exam). The paint engine is libmypaint 1.6.1 (ISC licence, github.com/mypaint/libmypaint; built with a one-line determinism
patch); the brush presets come from mypaint-brushes 2.0.2 (CC0-1.0; the release's COPYING and AUTHORS are in
/opt/mypaint/brushes/ in the image).
