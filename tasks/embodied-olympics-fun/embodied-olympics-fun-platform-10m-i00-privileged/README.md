## Task
Do a forward dive straight (101A) from the 10 m platform: from your standing start at its front end, take off, turn half a somersault forwards in the straight position and enter the water head first, vertical, with your arms stretched beyond your head. (Embodied Olympics `platform-10m`: one Unitree G1 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
A dive needs a take-off that is high enough and gives exactly the right forward rotation, then a straight body with the legs drawn together, the toes pointed and the short arms stretched overhead through a 1.4 s fall. A small error at the take-off becomes a short or over-rotated entry, and nothing can be corrected once it is in the air.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle.py` drives the robot through the task's own tooling, closed loop on the privileged state, and writes the trajectory (`solution/solve.sh`). Reference: a scripted G1 dive (stand with the arms overhead, a balanced crouch, a 0.37 s lean with soft ankles, a lowcmd push with the hips opened, a straight entry shape), the lean time found by bisection: a half somersault of 167 deg at the first touch, the body line 8.5 deg off vertical when the hips reach the surface, entry 2.29 m out. Referee test: 12 negatives, incl. over- and under-rotation, a topple, a scrape, arms down, legs apart, toes, a twist and a side entry (injected kicks), too far, a step, a hop (double jump). Measured: success after 6.32 s of the 20 s limit, live and in a fresh replay, ending in the same state.

## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in one fresh process (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success. The rules: You stand on the 10 m platform's front end facing the water (+x). The dive: take off from the platform (both feet stay on it until the take-off: a foot off it for more than 0.1 s while the other is on it, or any flight of 0.03 s or more that lands on the platform again, fails; the take-off counts when you are in the air and your centre of mass has risen at least 0.1 m above its height at the start), turn forwards through half a somersault (180.0 degrees, within 45.0, counted from your upright start) and enter the water head first. From the moment your body line (from the middle of your ankles to the middle of your shoulders) passes the horizontal until the dive is complete you must be in the straight position: hips and knees each bent less than 20.0 degrees from straight, the ankles at most 0.12 m apart, the toes pointed (each ankle pitched at least 0.3 rad towards the toes). The entry starts when the first part of you reaches the water surface (z = 0) and the dive is complete when the whole of you is under it; that first part must be a hand or the head (feet first fails); when your pelvis reaches the surface your body line must be within 15.0 degrees of vertical, head down; through the entry: not twisted (your shoulders' axis within 20.0 degrees of the platform's width direction); both palms at least 0.15 m beyond the centre of your head along the body line, at most 0.2 m apart and each within 0.15 m of the body line; and the straight position. The entry point (the middle of your palms when the first part of you touches the water) must be between 0.5 m and 3.0 m in front of the platform's front edge (closer is unsafely close) and within 0.5 m to either side of the line you face at the start. You fail if, after the take-off, any part of you touches the platform or its railings. Before the take-off any part other than the feet on the platform, or anything on the railings, fails. The water is not simulated: nothing after the dive is complete is judged. Success: a complete dive that keeps all of these. The attempt ends after 20 s.
