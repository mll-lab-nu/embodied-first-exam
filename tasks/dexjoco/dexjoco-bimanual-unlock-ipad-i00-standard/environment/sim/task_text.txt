Enter the passcode 1-2-3 on the iPad's screen (picking the iPad up is allowed, not required). A digit is pressed when a fingertip of the right hand (index, middle, ring or thumb) touches its button; touches by the left hand do not count; pressing any other digit than the next one of 1-2-3 ends the episode as a failure. Success: the screen is unlocked (1, 2, 3 in order) and stays unlocked for 10 consecutive control steps.

The iPad rests on a stand on the table, screen facing the robots; its keypad shows the digits.
