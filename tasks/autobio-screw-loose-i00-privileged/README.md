## Task
AutoBio's `screw_loose` (Unscrew centrifuge tube cap), with two ALOHA arms (`arm1` and `arm2`; 6 joints and a parallel gripper each), fixed to the bench on either side of the rack: Unscrew the cap of the 50 mL centrifuge tube and lift it off. Success at the first control step where the cap is more than 0.15 m above the tube body's origin (screwed on it sits 0.1197 m above it, so 3 cm up), `arm1`'s left finger (geom `1/aloha:leftfinger`) touches the cap (geom `6/cap_cyl`), and the cap's thread (geom `6/nut`) no longer touches the tube's (geom `7/bolt`).
On the bench: a rack with a 50 mL tube standing in it, its cap screwed on. The thread is a real one: the cap comes off only by turning it. AutoBio's prompt for this task: "one stabilizes tube while the other twists cap". The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 14)`, one row per 2 ms control step).

## Difficulty
AutoBio's level: medium. A real thread: the cap separates from the tube only after it has been turned off, and the check needs it held 3 cm up by the first arm. The agent has full access to the simulator and to AutoBio's task code, but not to its scripted expert: it has to build the motion itself from joint position targets at the physics rate.

## Reference solution
AutoBio's scripted expert (screw_loosen.py @ 0850421), seed 0: the position targets it sends, recorded at every physics step, clipped to the actuators' ranges, rounded to float32 (so the standard twin's client executes the same rows), cut at the first success: `solution/oracle.npz`, 13568 rows, success after 13568 control steps. Replayed and saved by `solution/solve.sh`; re-checked by `scripts/autobio/make_facts.py --check` (two fresh processes, same final state).

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the compiled model and the rebuilt start state are checked against its recorded hashes); pass iff both episodes succeed (the first control step at which AutoBio's check holds ends the episode in success) in the same state (`deterministic`). `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
