# kinder-obstruction-3d-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-obstruction-3d-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/Obstruction3D-o4-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: put the purple block on the thin purple pad on the wooden table; three of the four small red blocks sit on the pad and the fourth is elsewhere on the table. The task succeeds the moment the robot holds nothing, the block's underside is within 5 mm of the pad's top surface, and all four of its bottom corners lie within the pad's outline enlarged by 5 mm on every side.
