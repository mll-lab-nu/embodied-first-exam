## Task
AutoBio's `screw_tighten` (Screw on centrifuge tube cap), with two ALOHA arms (`arm1` and `arm2`; 6 joints and a parallel gripper each), fixed to the bench on either side of the rack: Screw the cap onto the 50 mL centrifuge tube. The tube stands open in the rack; its cap lies on the bench. Success at the first control step where the cap is less than 0.123 m above the tube body's origin (fully screwed on: 0.1197 m), the cap's axis is within 5 mm of the tube's, and the cap's thread (geom `6/nut`) touches the tube's (geom `7/bolt`).
On the bench: a rack with an open 50 mL tube standing in it, and the tube's screw cap lying on the bench. The thread is a real one: the cap goes on only by turning it. AutoBio's prompt for this task: "one grips tube while the other twists cap". The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 14)`, one row per 2 ms control step).

## Difficulty
AutoBio's level: hard. A real thread: the cap has to be picked off the bench, set on the tube within 5 mm of its axis and turned down to within 3.3 mm of fully on. The agent has full access to the simulator and to AutoBio's task code, but not to its scripted expert: it has to build the motion itself from joint position targets at the physics rate.

## Reference solution
AutoBio's scripted expert (screw_tighten.py @ 0850421), seed 0: the position targets it sends, recorded at every physics step, clipped to the actuators' ranges, rounded to float32 (so the standard twin's client executes the same rows), cut at the first success: `solution/oracle.npz`, 7638 rows, success after 7638 control steps. Replayed and saved by `solution/solve.sh`; re-checked by `scripts/autobio/make_facts.py --check` (two fresh processes, same final state).

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the compiled model and the rebuilt start state are checked against its recorded hashes); pass iff both episodes succeed (the first control step at which AutoBio's check holds ends the episode in success) in the same state (`deterministic`). `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
