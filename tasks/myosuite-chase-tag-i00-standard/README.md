## Task
MyoSuite `myoChallengeChaseTagP1-v0` (MyoLegs, 80 muscles) on the same frozen instance as `myosuite-chase-tag-i00-privileged`: Tag the opponent: bring your pelvis within 0.5 m of it (horizontal distance) within 20 s of simulated time (MyoChallenge 2023 ChaseTag P1, task CHASE). Failure (the episode ends): the time limit, leaving the 13 x 13 m arena, or the pelvis below 0.5 m (fallen).
A musculoskeletal body with two legs stands in a square arena; an opponent stands somewhere in it (in this frozen instance it does not move). Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the body through a client, with only its own proprioception and the scene's cameras, and 10 resets (only the last episode is graded).

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `myosuite-chase-tag-i00-privileged` (first-pass rating: hard). In standard mode the agent loses free resets, the simulator and the object state: it sees camera images and the limb's proprioception, acts in actuator-command chunks, and gets 10 resets, so it has to close the loop on what it sees and learn the body from a few real attempts.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: no positive example in standard mode yet: the privileged twin's (Claude Code + Claude Opus 5.5 (run `claude_code-claude_opus_5_5-default`, 2026-10-06), trial `myosuite-chase-tag-i00-privilege__6GUXvYA`) fails once its rows are rounded to the client's float32 (the walk no longer reaches the opponent within its 329 rows); the family's solvability rests on the privileged twin; human review in PR #65.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. No oracle; human review in PR #65. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.

### Change Log
- 2026-10-09: the standard service's `effort_<joint>` proprioception (`joint_effort`) reads the force the physics
  applied (MyoSuite's robot data), not the mirror's zero-command force; and `RCB_RESET_BUDGET`'s default 0 ->
  10 (owner decision; PROTOCOL §4.5, docs/benchmarks/myosuite.md), with `POST /reset` in the service. The
  scene, instance and pass rule are unchanged, so the task keeps its name; runs recorded before this date saw the wrong
  efforts and had no reset, and are reported separately.
- 2026-10-10: a malformed request field (a null or non-numeric `dt` / `seconds`, `cameras` that is not a list of names,
  a `note` that is not text) is answered with 400 before anything runs; before, it was a 500 that ended the episode
  (for `note`, after the chunk had run). `tests/grade.py` books a missing, unreadable or never-finalized `final.json`
  as `infra_error = 1` instead of the agent's 0, and `finalize.sh` writes `final.json` atomically. The scene, instance
  and pass rule are unchanged, so the task keeps its name; every verdict that was reached before is the same now.
- 2026-10-10 (board 0067, R2): the service no longer publishes the pose of a camera that rides on a body of the
  scene that is not the agent's own (decided from the model: the camera's body is a mocap body or sits behind a
  joint that is not the body's). Here that is `opponent_view`: it still gives images and depth, but no mount, no
  `extrinsic_cv` / `cam2world_gl` (null in `meta["cameras"]`, NaN per frame), and its body is no longer a
  `/kinematics` link. Before, its pose was that body's position plus a constant, which standard mode must not
  give (PROTOCOL §4.5); runs recorded before this date could read it and are reported with that note. The
  scene, instance and pass rule are unchanged, so the task keeps its name.
  The client (`environment/rcb_client.py`): `Robot()` waits up to 3600 s for the service (was 600 s; no effect
  here, the service is up in seconds) and prints its progress, and `cameras="name"` (a string) is one camera,
  no longer its characters.
- 2026-10-10 (board 0067, R2): the scene text said "an opponent moves in it"; in this frozen instance the opponent's
  policy is `stationary` (MyoChallenge draws it once), so the text now says that it stands still. Only the text
  changed: the instance, the tooling and the pass rule are the same.
