## Task
Catch and shoot: catch the chest pass that comes to you from the left wing between your two palms, then shoot from where you stand so that the ball enters the basket from above. (Embodied Olympics `catch-and-shoot-realgrasp`: one Unitree H1-2 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
The pass arrives at 7 m/s and flat palms cannot close around it: the two palms have to meet the ball from opposite sides as it arrives and squeeze it to a stop with nothing but friction, before it bounces out or touches the body. Then a 0.62 kg ball must leave the two palms at about 9 m/s on a steep arc towards a ring 5.8 m away, from a body that may not take a step. The built-in walking gait steps on the spot even at a zero command, so while the ball is in your hands its steps are counted like any others.

## Reference solution
None. No reference controller exists for this family yet: catch by injection (the real pass flies to the robot; at 0.6 m to its left the ball is put at rest between the open palms, which really close and hold it by friction); no real two-palm catch of the 7.2 m/s pass was found (the arms give way, the ball slides off the first palm within 0.1 s, also when slowed to 30 %). Shot by injection as in free-throw-realgrasp (real swing and release, velocity set at the release). Negatives: nop (the pass knocks the resting right palm and drops), a tap shorter than the catch time, a too-weak squeeze, the squeeze eased after the catch, held against the chest, walking with the ball, the pivot foot dragged, a third step after a catch in the air, touched before the pass, a pass off the thigh, the shot short or wide, touched again, from below, a fall.

## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in two fresh processes (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success in both
and both end in the same state. The rules: The official basket: a rigid ring of 0.45 m inner diameter (0.018 m metal), its top edge 3.05 m above the floor, its centre 0.375 m in front of a backboard 1.8 m wide and 1.05 m high whose lower edge is 2.9 m above the floor. The ball is a size-7 basketball (0.239 m across, 0.62 kg, it keeps 0.85 of its speed in a bounce on the floor); it flies in air (drag and spin lift). It is passed to you 2.0 s after the start: a chest pass of 8.0 m/s from 5.0 m away. Your hands have no fingers and no grip: you can hold the ball only by pressing it between your two flat palms, and only the force of your arms and friction hold it; it can slip out. A hand is the palm pad and the wrist; a hand is on the ball while it touches the ball, gaps shorter than 0.03 s included; the ball is in your hands while at least one hand is on it. In order: you catch the ball before it has touched the floor (the catch: both your hands have been on the ball together for 0.1 s without a break; touches before that, of one hand or both, are allowed); from the catch the ball stays in your hands until you let go of it (the shot's release: the moment no hand is on it; a ball that slips or drops out of your hands is released too); its centre goes down through the ring's plane inside the ring and on down to one ball diameter below it, which is the success. Travelling, from the catch to the shot's release: if both your feet are on the floor at the catch, the first foot you lift makes the other your pivot foot; the pivot foot, and before a foot is lifted either foot, may not slide more than 0.2 m along the floor (in the steps case below: any foot that is on the floor, from where it stood or was put down), and once it has left the floor no foot may touch the floor again before the ball has left your hands. If a foot is off the floor at the catch, you may take at most 2 steps (a step: a foot touching the floor after it has been off it for at least 0.03 s; both feet landing within 0.03 s of each other are one step; not the same foot twice in a row), and after them no foot may touch the floor again before the release. You fail if anything of you touches the ball before it is passed; if, from the pass until the release, the ball touches any part of you other than your hands; if the passed ball touches the floor before you have caught it; if you travel; if any part of you touches the ball more than 0.05 s after the shot's release (it cannot be taken again); if the ball touches the floor before it is through the ring; if the ball's centre comes up through the ring from below; if you fall before the ball is through (a fall: your pelvis lower than 0.35 m above the floor, or any part of you other than your feet on the floor); or after 15 s. No oracle; human review in the pull request that adds the benchmark (PROTOCOL §2.7).
