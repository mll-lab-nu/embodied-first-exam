## Task
Move the blocks to the other table and stack them on the green pad. (BiGym 2.0 `stack_blocks`; the robot as a service: its three onboard cameras and its proprioception only, one
episode.)

## Difficulty
Loco-manipulation over the longest horizon (175 s): carry three blocks from one table to a pad on another and stack them within 5 cm; a block on the floor ends the episode. In standard mode the agent sees only the robot's cameras (the head camera looks down at the hands at the
start) and proprioception, acts through the client, and gets one episode, no reset.

## Reference solution
None (as `bigym2-stack-blocks-i00-privileged`).

## Verification
The sim sidecar judges the live episode with BiGym's own success check (`is_success`: the task's success held for 1 s,
no fall) and writes that verdict to `/data/final.json` (`graded_by = "live"`); nothing is replayed to grade it (owner,
2026-10-17). The scene's start is checked against the frozen instance (`instance.json`'s `t0`) when an
episode begins (a mismatch is `instance_mismatch`, infrastructure). `tests/grade.py` reads `final.json`: pass iff the
live episode succeeded. The video (`replay.mp4`) is rendered from `trajectory.npz` in one pass after the verdict and
never changes it. Success condition: the three blocks form one stack on the green pad: the lowest touching the pad, each of the others touching the block below it, at least 0.03 m higher and within 0.05 m of it horizontally; no block held by a gripper. A block touching the floor ends the episode as a failure. No oracle; human review in PR #123 (PROTOCOL §4).
