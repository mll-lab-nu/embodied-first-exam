## Task
MyoSuite `myoChallengeTableTennisP1-v0` (MyoArm with torso, 273 muscles) on the same frozen instance as `myosuite-table-tennis-i00-privileged`: Return the served ball: hit it with the paddle so that it lands on the opponent's half of the table (MyoChallenge 2025 TableTennis P1's own success test on the ball's contact history: after the serve's bounce on your half, the paddle touches it once, then it touches the opponent's half, with no second paddle touch and no further bounce on your half). Failure (the episode ends): any other outcome of the rally, the ball falling below 0.3 m, or 3 s of simulated time.
You stand at a table-tennis table holding a paddle in the right hand; a ball is served toward you from the far side. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the body through a client in one episode, with only its own proprioception and the scene's cameras.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `myosuite-table-tennis-i00-privileged` (first-pass rating: hard). In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and the object state: it sees camera images and the limb's proprioception, acts in actuator-command chunks, and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what it sees and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: no positive example yet (not attempted); the task is reported separately until one exists (PROTOCOL §7, Open 4); human review in PR #TBD.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. No oracle; human review in PR #TBD. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
