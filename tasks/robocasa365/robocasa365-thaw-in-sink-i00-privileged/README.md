## Task
RoboCasa365's `ThawInSink` kitchen task with a Franka Panda arm on an Omron mobile base (with a torso lift): Pick the orange from the counter and place it in the sink. Then turn on the sink faucet. Produce one replayable trajectory of the robot's native controller commands on one frozen instance; graded on the state after the last action. Generated by `scripts/robocasa365/generate.py`; benchmark notes in `docs/benchmarks/robocasa365.md`.

## Difficulty
Full privileged state, unlimited resets and self-replay; a mobile manipulator in one of RoboCasa365's large, cluttered kitchens, long-horizon contact-rich manipulation with a 20 Hz controller. The standard twin removes the privileged state.

## Reference solution
Retargeted by PR #4 from official demonstration episode 0 (`demo_0`, 542 recorded actions; RoboCasa365's demonstrations use a different arm controller): it follows the demonstration's recorded arm joint positions as this controller's absolute joint targets (PR #4 method `demo_joint`). Executed from the frozen scene by the robot's own controller: 542 actions in `solution/oracle.npz`. Neither the demonstration nor the reference is in the image; the agent cannot reach them.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of the instance; `success = deterministic ∧ success on the final state ∧ not solved at the start`. Reference: two fresh-process replays end in the live run's state (hash `7ea13f1627d3…`) and succeed. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start.
