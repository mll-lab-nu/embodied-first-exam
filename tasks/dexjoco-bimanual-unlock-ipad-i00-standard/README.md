## Task
DexJoCo `bimanual_unlock_ipad` (two Franka Panda arms, each with a 16-DoF Allegro hand) on the same frozen instance as `dexjoco-bimanual-unlock-ipad-i00-privileged`: Pick up the iPad and enter the passcode 1-2-3 on its screen. A digit is pressed when a FINGERTIP touches its button; pressing any other digit than the next one of 1-2-3 ends the episode as a failure. Success: the screen is unlocked (1, 2, 3 in order) and stays unlocked for 10 consecutive control steps.
Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with the robot's own
sensors, calibrated cameras (RGB, optional depth) and the robot's kinematics.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success check as `dexjoco-bimanual-unlock-ipad-i00-privileged`. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the
simulator and the object poses: it sees camera images and proprioception, acts in chunks of tcp-target + hand-joint rows
(or joint targets), and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what the cameras show and recover from its own
mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle_trajectory.npz`, 392 rows, float32-exact)
through the client as `ee_pose` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded
trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference, and the episode ends with success after 392 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes
`final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live check succeeded, both replays succeed,
and they end in the same state. Oracle: success after 392 control steps.
