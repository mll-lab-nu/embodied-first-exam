Tower of Hanoi with three disks on three vertical posts in a row along y: the small disk is on post A (y > 0, the left arm's side), the medium disk on post B (middle), the large disk on post C (y < 0). Build the full tower on post C: large at the bottom, then medium, then small, posts A and B empty, every disk at rest and seated on the post (horizontally within 4 cm of it, at its stacking height within 1.2 cm), held for 20 consecutive control steps. A larger disk resting on a smaller one, or a disk resting on the table, for 10 consecutive steps ends the episode as a failure.

Two arms with a hand each stand side by side facing the table (right arm at y < 0, left arm at y > 0); the wooden Hanoi base with its three posts is in front of them. Each disk has a hole and slides over a post.
