| Time limit | 2 h |

## Task
Push the sled by its handles along your lane until all of it is over the line ahead.

## Success criteria
A push sled (5 kg in all, its runners sliding on turf with a friction coefficient of 0.45) stands with its front edge
on the start line, its two upright handles towards the robot; the finish line is 12.5 m beyond the start line, and
the lane runs 1 m to each side of its centre line. The handles cannot be gripped. The referee's stages, in order:
moved, line.
- Moved: the sled's rear has gone 0.1 m forward.
- Line: the whole sled, its rear included, is over the finish line. The success is called at that moment.

Fouls (each fails the attempt): any part of the robot other than a hand touches the sled; a hand touches the sled
anywhere but on a handle; any part of the sled goes outside the lane. Fall (a failure at any time): the pelvis below
0.5 m, or any part other than the feet on the ground. Time limit: 60 s (6,000 control steps).

Graded file: `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, as `s.save_trajectory()` writes it: an `actions` array of sim rows, one per
10 ms control step, and the task's family name. After the agent's session it is replayed once, open-loop, from the
frozen start, in a fresh process of a separate verifier container without network. Rows run one per control step
until the referee calls the attempt; rows after the call, and rows past the attempt's time limit, are not run. The
task is passed if the referee has called a success when the replay stops. If the rows run out before the referee's
call, the task is not passed. A row the robot cannot run (its first value, the mode, is not one of the robot's modes)
ends the replay at that row without a pass.

The physics diverging (a non-finite state, a bad acceleration, or the robot or a prop faster than 150 m/s) ends the
attempt as a failure, unless a success was called in an earlier control step. Only the trajectory is scored (`gen.py`
and `NOTES.md` are not). A missing file, a file that is not such an array (a link, over 64 MB, more than 40,000 rows,
the wrong row width), a file for another task, or a non-finite value in the rows that are used scores 0. An image that
no longer rebuilds the frozen start, or a replay that crashes or runs over 600 s, is an infrastructure failure, not a
score.
