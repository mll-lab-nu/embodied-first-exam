## Task
RoboCasa's `MicrowaveThawing` kitchen task with a Franka Panda arm on an Omron mobile base (with a torso lift): Pick the squash from the counter and place it in the microwave. Then turn on the microwave. Produce one replayable trajectory of the robot's native controller commands on one frozen instance; graded on the state after the last action. Generated by `scripts/robocasa/generate.py`; benchmark notes in `docs/benchmarks/robocasa.md`.

## Difficulty
Full privileged state, unlimited resets and self-replay; a mobile manipulator in a cluttered kitchen, long-horizon contact-rich manipulation with a 20 Hz controller. The standard twin removes the privileged state.

## Reference solution
The demonstration's own actions (original: 763 actions), executed from the frozen scene by the robot's own controller: 763 actions in `solution/oracle.npz`. The demonstration is a reference solution: it is not in the image and the agent cannot reach it.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of the instance; `success = deterministic ∧ success on the final state ∧ not solved at the start`. Reference: two fresh-process replays end in the live run's state (hash `bffb21e0022c…`) and succeed. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start.
