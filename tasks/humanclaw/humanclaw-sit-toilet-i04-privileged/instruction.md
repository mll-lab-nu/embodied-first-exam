# HumanCLAW · sit on the toilet, i04 (privileged)

## Task
A simulated adult human body in a furnished house (HumanCLAW: Habitat-Sim with Half-Physics, an HSSD scene); the body
moves only by motion skills: walk, turn, side step, step back, steps up or down, sit and stop. Task (HumanCLAW's own
words): Find the toilet and move until your body is touching the target, with zero distance to the target object.
Finally, sit on the toilet. The scene is one fixed instance. You have the full simulator to develop with; you hand in
the list of actions of one episode.

## Goal
Success: you have sat down on a toilet and are sitting on it, your pelvis resting on it at the end of your last motion,
when you stop (any toilet in the scene counts; at least one sit before the stop). The episode ends with your stop
(action 1), which declares that you are done: how things stand at that moment is what counts, and only a stop can
succeed. Ending without a stop fails: the 100th action ends the episode whatever it is.

Deliverable: `/app/output/trajectory.npz` with `actions`, a float array (T, 2), T ≤ 100, the episode's actions in order
(`Env.save()` writes it); it is replayed from the frozen start and scores 1 iff that replay succeeds. Only it is scored;
also leave `/app/output/gen.py`, the script that produced it (it must run and reproduce `trajectory.npz`, reading
nothing but `/app/instance.json` and files under `/app/output/`), and `/app/output/NOTES.md` (at most 20 lines: your
final approach and why it works, and what you tried that failed). Save a valid trajectory early and overwrite it when
you have a better one.

Rules: write your own controller; do not use the benchmark's demonstrations, trained policies or any published solution.

## Robot Interface
### Session
`hc_tools`, already installed (`python` from any directory):
```python
from hc_tools import Env
env = Env()                          # the frozen episode, on the GPU (~30 s to build)
env.step(0, 0.4)                     # one action; returns env.state()
env.state()                          # position, heading_deg, pelvis_rise_m, actions_used/left, done, success,
                                     # body_target_distance_m, sits_so_far, pelvis_touches_target
env.targets()                        # the target objects' centres and bounding boxes, the rule
env.shortest_path([x, y, z], agent_radius=0.35)  # Habitat navmesh path from the body to a point
                                     # (agent_radius: a navmesh for a wider body; the default is narrow)
env.rgb()                            # the head camera's image
env.reset()                          # back to the frozen start (a fresh simulator), any number of times
env.save("/app/output/trajectory.npz")
```
`python /opt/tools/hc_tools.py replay /app/output/trajectory.npz` replays a trajectory as the grader does (add
`--video out.mp4` to watch it). The motion model's noise is seeded at every reset, so the same actions from the start
give the same episode on this machine's GPU, as in the grader's replay; check it with a replay. The source of every
module is in `/opt/humanclaw/src/humanclaw_bench` and `/opt/tools`.

### Actions
An action is a row `[action_id, value]`:

| id | action | value |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | walk forward | stride in m: `0.2`, `0.4` or `0.6` (nothing else) |
| 1 | stop | `0`. Ends the episode: you declare you are done. Only a stop can succeed |
| 2 | turn in place | signed degrees, + left (counter-clockwise seen from above), - right; \|value\| clamped to [10, 120]; 0 is refused |
| 3 | climb up one step | `0` |
| 4 | sit down | target pelvis height in m, clamped to [0.15, 0.85] |
| 5 | step back | m, clamped to [0.10, 0.60] |
| 6 | side step | signed m, + left, - right; \|value\| clamped to [0.10, 0.50]; 0 is refused |
| 7 | walk down one step | `0` |

Each action except stop is one 0.5 s motion of the whole body (15 frames at 30 Hz), generated by HumanCLAW's motion
model and played out under physics: the body ends up close to, not exactly at, what was asked, and it can bump into
furniture (which can move).

## Limits
- Time: 3600 s of wall-clock time from the start of your session; everything counts (the simulator's start-up too). At
  the end you are stopped without warning and only what is in `/app/output/` at that moment is graded. Time left:
  `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json` (`time_left_s`, `deadline_utc`). Calls into a long-running process can block for their
  whole wait time: use short waits or one-shot scripts.
- Tokens: no separate limit.
- Network: your model's API and the package registries (PyPI, Debian / Ubuntu archives) only. You may install packages
  and read anything in your container, the simulator's and the benchmark's source included. Do not search for or
  download a ready-made solution (published or reference solutions, recorded demonstrations, trained policies); trials
  are reviewed.
- Resources: 6 CPUs, 8 GiB memory, 1024 processes and threads, 10 GiB disk (new files under `/app`, `/tmp`, `/root`),
  one GPU (8 GiB of its memory). Over a cap a process can be killed (the CPU cap only slows you down); usage:
  `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json`.
- Actions: at most 100 per episode, the stop included.
- Resets: as many as you like (`env.reset()`, a fresh simulator).
