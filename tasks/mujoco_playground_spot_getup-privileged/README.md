## Task
MuJoCo Playground `SpotGetup` (Boston Dynamics Spot), rebuilt in plain MuJoCo from Playground's scene files: Boston Dynamics Spot has fallen over. Make it get up and stand upright at its standing height.
It starts lying with its trunk tilted 128.4 deg (Playground's random drop, then a 0.5 s settle) and has 6 s to stand upright at its standing height. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets
(`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 12)`, 1 <= T <= 300).

## Difficulty
Recovery from a fall: the robot lies on its side or back and has to turn itself over with its legs and then stand up within 6 s, with the trunk, thighs and calves all in contact with the floor (full collisions). The motion is whole-body and contact-rich, and a turn that fails leaves it on its back. Spot's trunk alone weighs 33 kg and its legs are long: a turn has to be timed against gravity.

## Reference solution
A policy trained here on Playground's SpotGetup reward (PPO on the CPU, `scripts/mujoco-playground/train/`, not in the image), run closed loop until the check completes (`scripts/mujoco-playground/oracle.py`): upright after 63 control steps, IMU at 0.478 m. Replayed by `solution/solve.sh` (`solution/oracle.npy`, 63 rows); the rows are float32-exact, so the
standard twin's client executes the same targets. Re-verified by `scripts/mujoco-playground/select_instances.py`: two
fresh-process replays succeed and end in the same state.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own
copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the start state is checked against its hash on every load); pass iff both
succeed and end in the same state (`deterministic`). Oracle: success after 63 control steps. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
