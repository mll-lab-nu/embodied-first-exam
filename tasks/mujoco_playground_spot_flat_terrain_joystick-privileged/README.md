## Task
MuJoCo Playground `SpotFlatTerrainJoystick` (Boston Dynamics Spot), rebuilt in plain MuJoCo from Playground's scene files: Make Boston Dynamics Spot follow the joystick command schedule on flat ground for the whole 20 s episode without falling over.
For 20 s the robot gets a joystick command schedule (5 segments: planar velocity and yaw rate in its own frame) and must track it without falling over. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets
(`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 12)`, T = 1000 (the whole schedule)).

## Difficulty
Velocity tracking for 20 s: the agent has to make a 12-joint quadruped walk (a gait, not one motion) and steer it through the schedule's changes of speed, direction and turning rate, and an open-loop trajectory of 1000 steps only works if it is computed against the simulator. Spot is large and heavy, and Playground stops the episode once its trunk tilts by about 32 deg, so the gait has to stay level.

## Reference solution
A policy trained here on Playground's SpotFlatTerrainJoystick reward (PPO on the CPU, `scripts/mujoco-playground/train/`, not in the image), its targets recorded (`scripts/mujoco-playground/oracle.py`): worst segment errors 0.155 m/s and 0.072 rad/s. Replayed by `solution/solve.sh` (`solution/oracle.npy`, 1000 rows); the rows are float32-exact, so the
standard twin's client executes the same targets. Re-verified by `scripts/mujoco-playground/select_instances.py`: two
fresh-process replays succeed and end in the same state.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own
copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the start state is checked against its hash on every load); pass iff both
succeed and end in the same state (`deterministic`). Oracle: success, worst segment errors 0.155 m/s and 0.072 rad/s. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
