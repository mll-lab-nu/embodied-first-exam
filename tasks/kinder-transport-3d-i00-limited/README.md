# kinder-transport-3d-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-transport-3d-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/Transport3D-o2-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: put the yellow cube and the dark grey metal cube, both on the floor, and the open-topped box with a blue flower print onto the wooden table (its top 0.4 m above the floor). The task succeeds the moment the gripper is fully open (`gripper_pos` at most 0.0125) and the centres of both cubes and of the box are each at least 0.3 m above the floor and at least 0.2 m from the arm's end-effector point (`spec()["arm"]["end_effector"]`).
