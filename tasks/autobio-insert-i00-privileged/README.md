## Task
AutoBio's `insert` (Transfer centrifuge tube), with a UR5e arm (6 joints) with a Robotiq 2F-85 gripper, fixed to the bench: Move the 50 mL centrifuge tube from the rack it stands in to the other rack, into the slot at row 0, column 4 (AutoBio's indices: `s.task.rack2.get_position(s.data, 0, 4, '50ml')`). Success at the first control step where the tube's body is within 15 mm (horizontally) of that slot's centre and its height is between 0.829 and 0.831 m (standing in a slot it is at 0.830 m).
On the bench: two 10-slot tube racks (2 rows of 5 each) side by side, with one capped 50 mL tube standing in one of them. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 7)`, one row per 2 ms control step).

## Difficulty
AutoBio's level: medium. The tube is taken out of one slot and seated in a given slot of the other rack, within 15 mm of its centre and 1 mm of the seated height. The agent has full access to the simulator and to AutoBio's task code, but not to its scripted expert: it has to build the motion itself from joint position targets at the physics rate.

## Reference solution
AutoBio's scripted expert (transfer_centrifuge_tube.py @ 0850421), seed 0: the position targets it sends, recorded at every physics step, clipped to the actuators' ranges, rounded to float32 (so the standard twin's client executes the same rows), cut at the first success: `solution/oracle.npz`, 4162 rows, success after 4162 control steps. Replayed and saved by `solution/solve.sh`; re-checked by `scripts/autobio/make_facts.py --check` (two fresh processes, same final state).

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the compiled model and the rebuilt start state are checked against its recorded hashes); pass iff both episodes succeed (the first control step at which AutoBio's check holds ends the episode in success) in the same state (`deterministic`). `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
