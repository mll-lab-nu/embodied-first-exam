## Task
MyoSuite `myoChallengeChaseTagP1-v0` (MyoLegs, a free-standing musculoskeletal model of a human torso and both legs, 80 muscles): Tag the opponent: bring your pelvis within 0.5 m of it (horizontal distance) within 20 s of simulated time (MyoChallenge 2023 ChaseTag P1, task CHASE). Failure (the episode ends): the time limit, leaving the 13 x 13 m arena, or the pelvis below 0.5 m (fallen).
A musculoskeletal body with two legs stands in a square arena; an opponent stands somewhere in it (in this frozen instance it does not move). The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of actuator commands (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 80)`, one row per 10 ms control step).

## Difficulty
First-pass rating: hard. The action is muscle-level (80 muscles, each only pulling, several crossing more than one joint, with activation delays): the agent has to build its own joint control from muscle excitations. Success is the challenge's own event; nothing short of it counts, and the challenge's failure conditions and time limit apply.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: a successful model trial on this frozen instance: Claude Code + Claude Opus 5.5 (run `claude_code-claude_opus_5_5-default`, 2026-10-06), trial `myosuite-chase-tag-i00-privilege__6GUXvYA` (329 rows); today's verifier scores its trajectory 1 in two fresh trials (docs/benchmarks/myosuite.md); human review in PR #65.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.npz`; the rebuilt start state is checked against its recorded hash); pass iff both episodes end in success (the challenge's own success event) in the same state (`deterministic`). No oracle; human review in PR #65. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
