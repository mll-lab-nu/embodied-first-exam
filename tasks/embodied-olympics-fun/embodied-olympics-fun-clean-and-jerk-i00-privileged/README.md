## Task
Clean and jerk the barbell: lift it from the platform to your shoulders, then to locked arms overhead, and hold it there motionless until the down signal. (Embodied Olympics `clean-and-jerk`: one Unitree H1-2 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
The barbell weighs two thirds of the robot: held at the shoulders it asks about twice the torque the elbow motors give (18 N m), so the arms cannot simply carry it there. Nothing balances the robot under a load, and no built-in action bends down to the bar or straightens the knees.

## Reference solution
None. No reference controller exists for this family yet: positive by injection (a crane force on the 45 kg barbell and a balance force on the trunk; the final 2 s are carried by the robot's own locked arms and legs); negatives: the bar off the platform without both hands, let go, down before the signal, overhead without a clean, bent elbows or knees, split feet, another part on the platform. At the shoulders the elbows need 34 of their 18 N m; overhead with straight arms the robot holds the bar about 1 s.

## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in two fresh processes (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success in both
and both end in the same state. The rules: The barbell is the IWF men's bar (20.0 kg, 2.2 m long; the grip section between the sleeves is 1.31 m long and 0.028 m thick; the sleeves are 0.415 m long and 0.05 m thick and revolve on the bar) with one 10.0 kg rubber disc of 0.45 m and one 2.5 kg collar on each sleeve: 45.0 kg. It lies on the competition platform (4.0 m by 4.0 m, 0.1 m high), on which you stand. A hand can grip the grip section anywhere along it. Lift-off: the bar may leave the platform only in both hands: you fail the moment the bar's axis is more than 0.03 m above where it lies at rest while fewer than two hands grip it. The attempt: once the bar's centre has been 0.48 m above the platform (knee height) the lift must be finished: from then on you fail if either hand lets go or if any part of the barbell touches the platform before the down signal. The clean: the bar must first rest at your shoulders: its axis within 0.15 m of both shoulder joints, both hands on it, both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight, both feet on the platform and on one line, the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar (your trunk upright: the cosine of its tilt at least 0.94; the bar's centre no more than 0.25 m, horizontally, from the point midway between your shoulder joints; the bar, seen from above, within 20.0 degrees of the line through them), with the attempt begun (the bar has been above knee height and touches nothing but you), all of it without a break for 0.5 s. You fail if the bar's axis is more than 0.25 m above the centre of your head before that (a lift from the platform to overhead in one movement is a snatch). The jerk and the finish: after the clean, bring the bar overhead. The final position is: the bar's axis at least 0.25 m above the centre of your head, both hands on it, both elbows within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle between the upper arm's and the forearm's long axes), both knees within 10.0 degrees of straight (the angle hip, knee, ankle), both feet on the platform and on one line (their centres no more than 0.05 m apart along the horizontal direction square to the bar; under a bar tilted more than 60.0 degrees from level the feet never count as on one line), the bar and your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s, and you under the bar as in the clean (trunk upright, the bar's centre over the middle of your shoulders, the bar along your shoulder line). Once it is held for 2.0 s without a break the down signal is given: that is success, and the attempt ends. At any time you fail if a part of you other than your feet touches the platform, if any part of you or of the barbell touches the floor beside the platform, or after 60 s. There is no other fall rule: you may squat as low as you like, and the bar may touch your body. No oracle; human review in the pull request that adds the benchmark (PROTOCOL §2.7).
