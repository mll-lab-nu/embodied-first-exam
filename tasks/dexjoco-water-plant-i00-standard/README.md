## Task
DexJoCo `water_plant` (a Franka Panda arm with a 16-DoF Allegro hand) on the same frozen instance as `dexjoco-water-plant-i00-privileged`: Pick up the spray bottle, point its nozzle at the potted plant and squeeze the trigger. Success: the trigger is held pulled (trigger joint > 0.34 rad; it counts as released again below 0.25 rad) while the nozzle reference point is within 0.20 m of the plant horizontally and within +-0.20 m of the plant's height AND the spray points at the plant, all three for 30 consecutive control steps (0.6 s). The spray leaves the spray head horizontally forward: perpendicular to the bottle's long axis, on the side the trigger faces (the bottle's local +z; its long axis, bottom to head, is local +y). It points at the plant when that direction is within 45 degrees of the line from the nozzle to the plant, e.g. with the bottle held upright and its trigger side facing the plant. Pulling the trigger while the nozzle is outside the zone ends the episode as a failure.
Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with the robot's own
sensors, calibrated cameras (RGB, optional depth) and the robot's kinematics.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success check as `dexjoco-water-plant-i00-privileged`. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the
simulator and the object poses: it sees camera images and proprioception, acts in chunks of tcp-target + hand-joint rows
(or joint targets), and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what the cameras show and recover from its own
mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle_trajectory.npz`, 307 rows, float32-exact)
through the client as `ee_pose` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded
trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference, and the episode ends with success after 307 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes
`final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live check succeeded, both replays succeed,
and they end in the same state. Oracle: success after 307 control steps.
