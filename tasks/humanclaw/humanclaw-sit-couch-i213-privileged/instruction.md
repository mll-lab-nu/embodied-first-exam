# HumanCLAW · sit on the couch, i213 (privileged)

## Task
Sit down on any couch in the house, and stop while you are still sitting on it.

## Goal
Success: when you stop, you are sitting on a couch. The episode ends when you stop (action 1), which declares that you
are done. The state at that moment is what counts, and only a stop can succeed. Ending without a stop fails. The 100th
action ends the episode, whatever that action is.

Deliverable: `/app/output/trajectory.npz` with `actions`, a float array (T, 2), T ≤ 100: the episode's actions in order
(`Env.save()` writes it). It is replayed from the frozen start and scores 1 if and only if that replay succeeds. Only
`trajectory.npz` is scored. Also leave `/app/output/gen.py`, the script that produced it; it must run and reproduce
`trajectory.npz`, reading nothing but `/app/instance.json` and files under `/app/output/`. Also leave
`/app/output/NOTES.md`: at most 20 lines on your final approach and why it works, and what you tried that failed. Save a
valid trajectory early and overwrite it when you have a better one.

Rules: **No cheating.** Do not search for, download or use any existing solution (published or reference solutions, the
benchmark's demonstrations, trained policies); trials are reviewed.

## Robot Interface
A simulated adult human body in a furnished house (HumanCLAW: Habitat-Sim with Half-Physics, an HSSD scene); the body
moves only by motion skills: walk, turn, side step, step back, steps up or down, sit and stop. The scene is one fixed
instance. You have the full simulator to develop with; you hand in the list of actions of one episode.

### Session
`hc_tools`, already installed (`python` from any directory):
```python
from hc_tools import Env
env = Env()                          # the frozen episode, on the GPU (~30 s to build)
env.step(0, 0.4)                     # one action; returns env.state()
env.state()                          # position, heading_deg, pelvis_rise_m, actions_used/left, done, success,
                                     # body_target_distance_m, sits_so_far, pelvis_touches_target
env.targets()                        # the target objects' centres and bounding boxes, the rule
env.shortest_path([x, y, z], agent_radius=0.35)  # Habitat navmesh path from the body to a point
                                     # (agent_radius: a navmesh for a wider body; the default is narrow)
env.rgb()                            # the head camera's image
env.reset()                          # back to the frozen start (a fresh simulator), any number of times
env.save("/app/output/trajectory.npz")
```
`python /opt/tools/hc_tools.py replay /app/output/trajectory.npz` replays a trajectory as the grader does (add
`--video out.mp4` to watch it). The motion model's noise is seeded at every reset. So on this machine's GPU, the same
actions from the start give the same episode, as in the grader's replay. Check this with a replay. The source of every
module is in `/opt/humanclaw/src/humanclaw_bench` and `/opt/tools`.

### Actions
An action is a row `[action_id, value]`:

| id | action | value |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | walk forward | stride in m: `0.2`, `0.4` or `0.6` (nothing else) |
| 1 | stop | `0`. Ends the episode: you declare you are done. Only a stop can succeed |
| 2 | turn in place | signed degrees, + left (counter-clockwise seen from above), - right; \|value\| clamped to [10, 120]; 0 is refused |
| 3 | climb up one step | `0` |
| 4 | sit down | target pelvis height in m, clamped to [0.15, 0.85] |
| 5 | step back | m, clamped to [0.10, 0.60] |
| 6 | side step | signed m, + left, - right; \|value\| clamped to [0.10, 0.50]; 0 is refused |
| 7 | walk down one step | `0` |

Each action except stop is one 0.5 s motion of the whole body (15 frames at 30 Hz), generated by HumanCLAW's motion
model and played out under physics. The body ends up close to, but not exactly at, what was asked. It can bump into
furniture, and the furniture can move.

## Limits
- Time: 3600 s of wall-clock time from the start of your session. Everything counts, including the simulator's start-up.
  At the end you are stopped without warning and only what is in `/app/output/` at that moment is graded. Time left:
  `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json` (`time_left_s`, `deadline_utc`). A call into a long-running process can block for its
  whole wait time. Use short waits or one-shot scripts.
- Tokens: no separate limit.
- Network: your model's API and the package registries (PyPI, Debian / Ubuntu archives) only. You may install packages
  and read anything in your container, including the simulator's and the benchmark's source.
- Resources: 6 CPUs, 8 GiB memory, 1024 processes and threads, 10 GiB disk (new files under `/app`, `/tmp`, `/root`),
  one GPU (8 GiB of its memory). Going over a cap can get a process killed (the CPU cap only slows you down). Usage:
  `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json`.
- Actions: at most 100 per episode, the stop included.
- Resets: as many as you like (`env.reset()`, a fresh simulator).
