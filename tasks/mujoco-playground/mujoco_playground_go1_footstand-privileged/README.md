## Task
MuJoCo Playground `Go1Footstand` (Unitree Go1), rebuilt in plain MuJoCo from Playground's scene files: Make the Unitree Go1 stand up on its hind legs, nose up, front legs in the air, and balance there for 2 s.
From standing, it has 10 s to get into a footstand on its hind legs and hold it for 2 s (Playground's footstand reward aims at the IMU 0.53 m high). The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets
(`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 12)`, 1 <= T <= 500).

## Difficulty
Dynamic balance on two legs: the robot has to throw its weight onto its hind legs, lift the other pair and then balance an inverted, top-heavy body on two small feet with 35 N m/rad position drives, correcting continuously; a front leg touching the floor or a tip-over ends it. Playground's footstand is trained with 100 M steps of PPO.

## Reference solution
A policy trained here on Playground's Go1Footstand reward (PPO on the CPU, `scripts/mujoco-playground/train/`, not in the image) with three training-only changes (`train/qenv.py` Go1FootstandStrict3): this task's hind-leg termination (with Playground's rule alone it learns to sit on a hind hip), half of the episodes starting in Playground's `footstand` keyframe, and a steeper height reward (with Playground's it settles in a squat at 0.2 m), run closed loop with Playground's action semantics (targets accumulate) until the hold completes (`scripts/mujoco-playground/oracle.py`): 113 control steps, pose angle 9.6 deg and IMU at 0.541 m at the end. Replayed by `solution/solve.sh` (`solution/oracle.npy`, 113 rows); the rows are float32-exact, so the
standard twin's client executes the same targets. Re-verified by `scripts/mujoco-playground/select_instances.py`: two
fresh-process replays succeed and end in the same state.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own
copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the start state is checked against its hash on every load); pass iff both
succeed and end in the same state (`deterministic`). Oracle: success after 113 control steps. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
