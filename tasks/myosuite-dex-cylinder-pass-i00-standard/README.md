## Task
MyoSuite `MyoHandCylindersmallPass-v0` (MyoHand, 39 muscles) on the same frozen instance as `myosuite-dex-cylinder-pass-i00-privileged`: Move the small cylinder along MyoDex's recorded pass (pick it up and present it for a hand-over) motion: a reference of the object's pose over 86 control steps (1.72 s), from GRAB motion capture (TCDM / MyoDex). The episode lasts exactly those 86 steps. Success: the object's motion follows the reference's: its centre of mass's displacement since the start within 0.03 m of the reference's displacement since the reference's start, at at least 90% of the control steps and at the last one, with the displacement error never above 0.05 m. Failure (the episode ends early): MyoDex's termination, the object more than 0.25 m from the reference or from the wrist.
A small cylinder lies on the table; the MyoHand is at its pre-grasp pose. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the body through a client in one episode, with only its own proprioception and the scene's cameras.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `myosuite-dex-cylinder-pass-i00-privileged` (first-pass rating: hard). In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and the object state: it sees camera images and the limb's proprioception, acts in actuator-command chunks, and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what it sees and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. No oracle; human review in PR #65. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
