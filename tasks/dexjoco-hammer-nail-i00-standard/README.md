## Task
DexJoCo `hammer_nail` (a Franka Panda arm with a 16-DoF Allegro hand) on the same frozen instance as `dexjoco-hammer-nail-i00-privileged`: Pick up the hammer and drive the nail into the wooden block by striking its head. A strike counts only if the hammer face was moving down faster than 0.02 m/s just before it touched the nail; each counted strike drives the nail 8 mm x min(3, speed / 0.02) deeper (at most 7.26 cm). Success: the nail is at least 4 cm deep.
Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with the robot's own
sensors, calibrated cameras (RGB, optional depth) and the robot's kinematics.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success check as `dexjoco-hammer-nail-i00-privileged`. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the
simulator and the object poses: it sees camera images and proprioception, acts in chunks of tcp-target + hand-joint rows
(or joint targets), and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what the cameras show and recover from its own
mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle_trajectory.npz`, 202 rows, float32-exact)
through the client as `ee_pose` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded
trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference, and the episode ends with success after 202 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook
(`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes
`final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live check succeeded, both replays succeed,
and they end in the same state. Oracle: success after 202 control steps.
