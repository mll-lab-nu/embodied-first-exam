# Geometry and reference notices

Rack, net, counter and room geometry are project-authored procedural approximations.
The maker catalogue, manual and photos are read-only research evidence, not
redistributed textures or CAD. No third-party visual asset is bundled.

The optional meta_actions helper is copied unchanged from the project platform
with its README and force code. Original robot assets and older hand-trial
deliveries remain read-only and are omitted from this explicit meta view.
