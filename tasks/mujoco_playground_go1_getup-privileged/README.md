## Task
MuJoCo Playground `Go1Getup` (Unitree Go1), rebuilt in plain MuJoCo from Playground's scene files: The Unitree Go1 has fallen over. Make it get up and stand upright at its standing height.
It starts lying with its trunk tilted 110 deg (Playground's random drop, then a 0.5 s settle) and has 6 s to stand upright at its standing height. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets
(`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 12)`, 1 <= T <= 300).

## Difficulty
Recovery from a fall: the robot lies on its side or back and has to turn itself over with its legs and then stand up within 6 s, with the trunk, thighs and calves all in contact with the floor (full collisions). The motion is whole-body and contact-rich, and a turn that fails leaves it on its back. A trained policy reaches a level crouch easily; standing up from it at the right moment is the rest.

## Reference solution
A policy trained here on Playground's Go1Getup reward (PPO on the CPU, `scripts/mujoco-playground/train/`, not in the image) turns the robot belly-down and level; then the joint targets go linearly to the `home` pose in 1 s and hold until the check completes (`scripts/mujoco-playground/oracle.py`): upright after 136 control steps, IMU at 0.257 m. Replayed by `solution/solve.sh` (`solution/oracle.npy`, 136 rows); the rows are float32-exact, so the
standard twin's client executes the same targets. Re-verified by `scripts/mujoco-playground/select_instances.py`: two
fresh-process replays succeed and end in the same state.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own
copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the start state is checked against its hash on every load); pass iff both
succeed and end in the same state (`deterministic`). Oracle: success after 136 control steps. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
