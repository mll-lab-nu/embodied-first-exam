## Task
RoboCasa365's `DeliverBrewedCoffee` kitchen task with a Franka Panda arm on an Omron mobile base (with a torso lift): Pick up the brewed coffee from the coffee machine and place it next to the breakfast plate on the dining counter. Produce one replayable trajectory of the robot's native controller commands on one frozen instance; graded on the state after the last action. Generated by `scripts/robocasa365/generate.py`; benchmark notes in `docs/benchmarks/robocasa365.md`.

## Difficulty
Full privileged state, unlimited resets and self-replay; a mobile manipulator in one of RoboCasa365's large, cluttered kitchens, long-horizon contact-rich manipulation with a 20 Hz controller. The standard twin removes the privileged state.

## Reference solution
Retargeted by PR #4 from official demonstration episode 0 (`demo_0`, 441 recorded actions; RoboCasa365's demonstrations use a different arm controller): it follows the demonstration's recorded arm joint positions as this controller's absolute joint targets, with the base and torso commands corrected by feedback (PR #4 method `demo_feedback`). Executed from the frozen scene by the robot's own controller: 441 actions in `solution/oracle.npz`. Neither the demonstration nor the reference is in the image; the agent cannot reach them.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays in fresh processes from the verifier's own copy of the instance; `success = deterministic ∧ success on the final state ∧ not solved at the start`. Reference: two fresh-process replays end in the live run's state (hash `f8925cfae582…`) and succeed. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0; the instance is not solved at the start.
