# kinder-sweep-into-drawer-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-sweep-into-drawer-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/SweepIntoDrawer3D-o5-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: get the five small dark-red cubes on top of the kitchen island into the middle drawer of the island's upper row of drawers, which starts closed; a wiper lies next to the cubes. The task succeeds the moment all five cubes' centres are inside that drawer, each at least 2.5 cm from the drawer's two side walls.
