## Task
ABC Sim `count_into_opaque_box` (two I2RT YAM arms (6 revolute joints and a parallel two-finger gripper each) side by side at the near edge of a table, facing +x (the left arm is the one at +y)): Put all the yellow triangular pieces in the box, and nothing else.
Twelve small objects in several shapes and colours, some larger than others, lie on the table next to an opaque box. The box's lid has a slot covered by a light spring-hinged flap: objects go in through the slot. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory (`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 14)`, one row per 0.034 s control step).

## Difficulty
First-pass rating: medium. Grounding a counting directive in what the cameras show, and dropping small objects through a narrow slot covered by a flap into a box whose contents can no longer be seen. The action is joint-level (two 6-joint arms and two grippers; no motion primitives), and the goal has to hold, so passing through it does not count.

## Reference solution
The commanded actions of one of upstream's validation demonstrations of this task, replayed from the frozen instance and cut at the success step: `solution/oracle.npy`, 277 rows (float32 values, so the standard twin's client sends the same rows), success after the last one. Replayed, checked and saved by `solution/solve.sh`.

## Verification
`/opt/abc-tools/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: one replay of `/app/output/trajectory.npz`, in a fresh process, from the verifier's own copy of the instance (`tests/instance.npz`; the rebuilt start state is checked against its recorded hash); pass iff the episode ends in success. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
