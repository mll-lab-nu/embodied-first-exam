## Task
Open the top drawer of the kitchen cabinet. (BiGym 2.0 `drawer_top_open`; the robot as a service: its three onboard cameras and its proprioception only, one
episode.)

## Difficulty
Grasp a drawer handle and pull the drawer at least 90% open while the controller keeps the robot standing against the pull. In standard mode the agent sees only the robot's cameras (the head camera looks down at the hands at the
start) and proprioception, acts through the client, and gets one episode, no reset.

## Reference solution
None (as `bigym2-drawer-top-open-i00-privileged`).

## Verification
The sim sidecar judges the live episode with BiGym's own success check (`is_success`: the task's success held for 1 s,
no fall) and writes that verdict to `/data/final.json` (`graded_by = "live"`); nothing is replayed to grade it (owner,
2026-10-17). The scene's start is checked against the frozen instance (`instance.json`'s `t0`) when an
episode begins (a mismatch is `instance_mismatch`, infrastructure). `tests/grade.py` reads `final.json`: pass iff the
live episode succeeded. The video (`replay.mp4`) is rendered from `trajectory.npz` in one pass after the verdict and
never changes it. Success condition: the top drawer is out to within 10% of its full travel (within about 0.038 m of fully open). No oracle; human review in PR #123 (PROTOCOL §4).
