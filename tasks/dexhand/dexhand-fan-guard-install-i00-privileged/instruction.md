# DexHand · Install a desk fan front guard (privileged)

## Task
Install the front guard on the desk fan.

## Goal
Success: the guard sits centred and square on the fan's rear rim, dome facing out, with all four clips closed and locked
over both rims (each upper clip locked after the guard was seated; the lower clips may be closed before or after); then
the hand touches neither the guard, the clips nor the fan, and the guard is still for a second. A replay ends as soon as
the task is done; later rows are not run.

Any of these ends the episode at once as a failure, which later actions cannot undo:
- The fan is moved or tilted.
- The guard, once lifted, touches the worktop, a table leg or the floor again.
- The guard is dropped, even if caught again.
- The guard touches the fan's base, stem, neck, motor housing, feet or buttons.
- The guard falls below the worktop or leaves the work area.
- The physics becomes unstable.

Deliverable: `/app/output/trajectory.npz` with a finite numeric `actions` array of shape `(T, 28)` for `sharpa` or
`(T, 26)` for `wuji`, at most 6000 rows, plus the `hand` member (`s.save_trajectory` writes both), and
`/app/output/gen.py`, the script that produced it. Save it compressed with `numpy.savez_compressed` (`s.save_trajectory`
does this). An archive larger than 64 MB is rejected. Save a valid one early and overwrite it when you have a better
one.

Rules: **No cheating.** Do not search for, download or use any existing solution (published or reference solutions, the
benchmark's demonstrations, trained policies); trials are reviewed.

Only the robot's actuator targets act on the scene: no object teleports, grasp welds, scripted object motion or applied
forces.

## Robot Interface
One dexterous hand on a six-axis wrist stage (no arm), at a worktop (DexHand, MuJoCo). The scene is one fixed instance.
You have the full simulator to develop with; you hand in a trajectory of motor targets.

### Session
`/opt/tools/rcb_dexhand.py`, importable as `rcb_dexhand`:
```python
from rcb_dexhand import Session
s = Session("fan_guard_install")                # default hand: DEXHAND_HAND or "sharpa"
s = Session("fan_guard_install", hand="wuji")   # explicit hand
s.hand, s.action_dim, s.actuator_names          # selected hand, row width and exact row order
s.hold_action()                                 # current motor targets
s.model.actuator_ctrlrange                      # native target limits
s.model, s.data                                 # privileged MuJoCo model and state
s.features()                                    # seat errors, clip angles, contacts, guard speed, failure details
s.status()                                      # success, done, reason, steps, stage (0..3), total_stages
s.step(row)                                     # ten 2 ms physics substeps; a no-op once the episode is done
s.render_frame("overview")                      # RGB uint8 (480, 640, 3); also "closeup"
s.reset()                                       # exact frozen reset
s.replay(actions)                               # reset, run rows until the episode ends, return status()
s.save_trajectory(actions, "/app/output/trajectory.npz")
```
You may read the complete simulator state (object poses, contacts, clip angles, `s.features()`); the scene facts are the
frozen values.

### Actions
Each row is one 50 Hz control step. The first six entries are the wrist-stage targets, identical for both hands:
`wrist_x, wrist_y, wrist_z` (metres, world frame) and `wrist_rx, wrist_ry, wrist_rz` (radians, serial XYZ joints, not a
quaternion). The finger targets follow in native actuator order, in radians:

- `sharpa`: 28 values, the six wrist targets and 22 Sharpa Wave finger targets `right_thumb_CMC_FE` ...
  `right_pinky_DIP`.
- `wuji`: 26 values, the same six wrist targets and 20 Wuji Hand 2.2 finger targets `r_wuji_r_THJ0..3`,
  `r_wuji_r_FFJ0..3`, `r_wuji_r_MFJ0..3`, `r_wuji_r_RFJ0..3`, `r_wuji_r_LFJ0..3`.

Every target must lie within `s.model.actuator_ctrlrange`. Both hands start from the same wrist targets; the frozen
finger start pose of each hand is in `task.json` (`initial_targets`).

### Hands
Two hand models: `sharpa` (Sharpa Wave, 22 finger actuators; the default) and `wuji` (Wuji Hand 2.2, 20 finger
actuators); the task is the same for both. Choose the hand with `Session(..., hand="wuji")` or the environment variable
`DEXHAND_HAND=wuji` (default `sharpa`). `s.save_trajectory(actions, path)` records the hand in the archive as the `hand`
member; an archive without it counts as `sharpa`. A trajectory whose row width does not match its hand is rejected.
Action rows are `28` wide for `sharpa` and `26` wide for `wuji`; `s.actuator_names` gives the exact order. Both hands
hang from the same six-axis wrist stage, with the same wrist joints, ranges, gains and start targets.

### Robot
You control one dexterous hand mounted on a six-DoF wrist positioning stage (three prismatic and three serial revolute
joints, no arm). The stage and the fingers are position actuated in the physics engine under native force bounds;
grasping, carrying and releasing require physical contact. The controller runs at 50 Hz, with ten 2 ms physics substeps.

The wrist targets `wrist_x, wrist_y, wrist_z` are world positions of the hand mount (metres) and
`wrist_rx, wrist_ry, wrist_rz` serial XYZ joint angles (radians, not a quaternion).

### Scene
World frame, metres and radians, z up.

- Worktop: top at z = 0.65, spanning x in [0.28, 1.08], y in [-0.50, 0.50].
- Fan (free body on four rubber feet, 2.15 kg with its clips): body frame starts at (0.73, 0.18, 0.952) with the cage
  axis along world -x. The rear rim (radius 0.1845, wire radius 0.0018) lies in the plane x = 0.73.
- Clip hinges (fixed to the fan; hinge axis tangential to the rim; angle 0 closed over both rims, 1.40 open, the elastic
  detent's over-centre point at 0.70): 45 deg (0.734, 0.3128, 1.0848), 135 deg (0.734, 0.0472, 1.0848), 225 deg (0.734,
  0.0472, 0.8192), 315 deg (0.734, 0.3128, 0.8192). All four clips start open at 1.40. A closed lower clip (225 or 315
  deg) forms an open-topped U channel that can carry the guard's rim.
- Front guard (rigid, 0.185 kg): rim radius 0.1845 (wire radius 0.0018), dish 0.047 deep toward the operator, a hub cap
  of radius 0.043 and height 0.022 at its centre (front face 0.057 in front of the rim plane), centre of mass 0.018 in
  front of the rim plane on the axis. It starts flat on the worktop, dome up, rim centre at (0.48, -0.275, 0.6518).
- Seat: the guard is seated with its centre at (0.72635, 0.18, 0.952), i.e. 3.65 mm in front of the rear-rim plane, axis
  along world -x.
- Wrist stage: targets are world positions x in [-0.2, 1.0], y in [-0.6, 0.6], z in [0.35, 1.55] and serial XYZ angles
  in [-3.14, 3.14]; it starts at (0.40, 0.00, 0.95, 3.0, 0, 0) (palm down, fingers along +x).
- Cameras (640 x 480): `overview` at (-0.06, -0.84, 1.40) looking at (0.60, -0.07, 0.84), fovy 45; `closeup` at (0.20,
  -0.27, 1.22) looking at (0.72, 0.17, 1.00), fovy 55.

## Limits
- Time: 3600 s of wall-clock time from the start of your session; everything counts (the simulator's start-up too). At
  the end you are stopped without warning and only what is in `/app/output/` at that moment is graded. Time left:
  `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json` (`time_left_s`, `deadline_utc`). Calls into a long-running process can block for their
  whole wait time: use short waits or one-shot scripts.
- Tokens: no separate limit.
- Network: your model's API and the package registries (PyPI, Debian / Ubuntu archives) only. You may install packages
  and read anything in your container, the simulator's and the benchmark's source included.
- Resources: 2 CPUs, 7 GiB memory, 2048 processes and threads, 10 GiB disk (new files under `/app`, `/tmp`, `/root`), no
  GPU. Over a cap a process can be killed (the CPU cap only slows you down); usage: `/run/rcb-limits/usage.json`.
- Resets: as many as you like (`s.reset()`).
