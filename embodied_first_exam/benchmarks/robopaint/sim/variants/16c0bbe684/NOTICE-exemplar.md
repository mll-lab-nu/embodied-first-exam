Modification notice (Arphic Public License, section 2a): on 2026-09-28 RoboPaint (Embodied First Exam) copied the characters'
outlines from `graphics.txt` unmodified and added only their placement on the paper (`truth.box_mm` / `truth.glyphs` in the graders'
instance.json); `target.png` (the exemplar as a picture) is a rendering of those outlines. These files are available under the
same licence, like the rest of this task directory.
