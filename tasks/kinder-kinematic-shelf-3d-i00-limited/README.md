# kinder-kinematic-shelf-3d-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-kinematic-shelf-3d-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/KinematicShelf3D-o3-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: put the three small book-textured blocks lying on the floor, red, blue and cream, on the dark-wood bookshelf standing against the wall next to the white door. The task succeeds the moment the gripper is fully open (`gripper_pos` at most 0.0125) and every block's centre is at least 0.224 m above the floor.

Generated by `scripts/kinder/gen_tasks.py` from `scripts/kinder/subset.toml`; see `docs/benchmarks/kinder.md`.
