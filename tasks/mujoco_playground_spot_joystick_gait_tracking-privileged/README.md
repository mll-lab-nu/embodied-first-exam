## Task
MuJoCo Playground `SpotJoystickGaitTracking` (Boston Dynamics Spot), rebuilt in plain MuJoCo from Playground's scene files: Make Boston Dynamics Spot follow the joystick command schedule for the whole 20 s episode with the commanded gait (the feet's swing pattern, frequency and height) without falling over.
For 20 s the robot gets a joystick command schedule (5 segments: planar velocity and yaw rate in its own frame) and must track it without falling over. Its feet must follow a pace gait. The deliverable is one replayable trajectory of joint position targets
(`/app/output/trajectory.npz`, `(T, 12)`, T = 1000 (the whole schedule)).

## Difficulty
Velocity tracking for 20 s: the agent has to make a 12-joint quadruped walk (a gait, not one motion) and steer it through the schedule's changes of speed, direction and turning rate, and an open-loop trajectory of 1000 steps only works if it is computed against the simulator. On top of the velocity commands the feet have to follow a commanded gait: which feet swing together, at a fixed frequency and to a commanded height.

## Reference solution
A policy trained here on Playground's SpotJoystickGaitTracking reward (PPO on the CPU, `scripts/mujoco-playground/train/`, not in the image), with the command added to its observation (`train/qenv.py` SpotJoystickGaitTrackingCmd: Playground's observation leaves the command out, so a policy trained on it marches in place and cannot track the joystick its reward asks for), its targets recorded (`scripts/mujoco-playground/oracle.py`): worst segment errors 0.183 m/s and 0.136 rad/s, foot height 0.038 m. Replayed by `solution/solve.sh` (`solution/oracle.npy`, 1000 rows); the rows are float32-exact, so the
standard twin's client executes the same targets. Re-verified by `scripts/mujoco-playground/select_instances.py`: two
fresh-process replays succeed and end in the same state.

## Verification
`tests/grade.py` in a separate sandbox without network: two replays, each in a fresh process, from the verifier's own
copy of the instance (`tests/instance.json`; the start state is checked against its hash on every load); pass iff both
succeed and end in the same state (`deterministic`). Oracle: success, worst segment errors 0.183 m/s and 0.136 rad/s. `nop`: no trajectory, reward 0.
