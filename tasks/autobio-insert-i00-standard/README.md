## Task
AutoBio's `insert` (Transfer centrifuge tube) on the same frozen instance as `autobio-insert-i00-privileged`: Move the 50 mL centrifuge tube (orange screw cap) from the rack it stands in to the other rack, into the slot AutoBio calls row 0, column 4 of that rack: of its two rows the one farther from the robot's base, and in that row the end slot on the side of the first rack. Success the moment the tube stands in that slot: within 15 mm of the slot's centre and seated at the height at which a tube stands in a slot (within 1 mm).
On the bench: two 10-slot tube racks (2 rows of 5 each) side by side, with one capped 50 mL tube standing in one of them. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the robot through a client in one episode, with only its proprioception and the scene's cameras.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `autobio-insert-i00-privileged` (AutoBio's level: medium). The tube is taken out of one slot and seated in a given slot of the other rack, within 15 mm of its centre and 1 mm of the seated height. In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and the state of the tubes and instruments: it sees camera images and the robot's proprioception, acts in joint-position chunks, and gets one episode, so it has to find the things it handles in the images and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_stream.py` streams the privileged reference's rows (`solution/oracle.npz`, 4162 rows, float32-exact) through the client in `hold` chunks; it uses no privileged information at run time. The service's recorded trajectory is then exactly the privileged reference: success after 4162 control steps.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
