## Task
Pick the cardboard box up from the side table and gently set it down on the kitchen counter. (BiGym 2.0 `pick_box`; the robot as a service: its three onboard cameras and its proprioception only, one
episode.)

## Difficulty
Whole-body carry: squat to a low side table, lift a 3 kg box with two palmless grippers, turn and walk, set it on the counter. In standard mode the agent sees only the robot's cameras (the head camera looks down at the hands at the
start) and proprioception, acts through the client, and gets one episode, no reset.

## Reference solution
None (as `bigym2-pick-box-i00-privileged`).

## Verification
The sim sidecar judges the live episode with BiGym's own success check (`is_success`: the task's success held for 1 s,
no fall) and writes that verdict to `/data/final.json` (`graded_by = "live"`); nothing is replayed to grade it (owner,
2026-10-17). The scene's start is checked against the frozen instance (`instance.json`'s `t0`) when an
episode begins (a mismatch is `instance_mismatch`, infrastructure). `tests/grade.py` reads `final.json`: pass iff the
live episode succeeded. The video (`replay.mp4`) is rendered from `trajectory.npz` in one pass after the verdict and
never changes it. Success condition: the box rests on the kitchen counter's top: touching it, its centre above the counter top, its lowest point within 0.03 m of the top's height; held by neither gripper. No oracle; human review in PR #123 (PROTOCOL §4).
