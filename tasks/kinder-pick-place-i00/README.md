# kinder-pick-place-i00

KinDER `kinder/PickPlace3D-o1-v0` (Dynamic3D, TidyBot++), seed 0, full mode: the agent has the simulator and its state and
hands in one trajectory, replayed open-loop in a fresh process. Its limited-mode twin is `kinder-pick-place-i00-limited`.

Task: get the small dark-red cube on the floor in front of the robot into a square on the floor that is not marked: x from 0.95 to 1.25 m and y from 0.35 to 0.65 m in KinDER's world frame (the frame of the state's positions; about 1.3 m ahead of the robot's start and 0.4 m to its left). The task succeeds the moment the cube's centre is inside that square and at most 10 cm above the floor.

Generated by `scripts/kinder/gen_tasks.py` from `scripts/kinder/subset.toml`; see `docs/benchmarks/kinder.md`.
