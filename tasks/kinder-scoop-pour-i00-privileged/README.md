## Task
KinDER `kinder/ScoopPour3D-o30-v0` (Dynamic3D, TidyBot++), seed 0, privileged mode: the agent has the simulator and its state and
hands in one trajectory, replayed open-loop in a fresh process. Its standard-mode twin is `kinder-scoop-pour-i00-standard`.

Task: move all 30 small dark-red cubes from the yellow bin into the green bin; a scoop is in the scene. The task succeeds the moment every cube's centre is inside the green bin, at least 1 cm from each of its inner walls and at least 0.5 cm below its rim.

## Difficulty
Nonprehensile, tool use, dynamics: move 30 small cubes from one bin to another with a scoop, each ending well inside the target bin.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability rests on the positive example in `oracle_evidence` (task.toml), checked by a human reviewer (docs/PROTOCOL.md §2.7).

## Verification
Two open-loop replays of `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from `tests/instance.json` in a fresh process, in a container of
their own (`tests/Dockerfile`, no network): pass iff both reach the goal within 1000 control steps and end in
the same state. No oracle; human review in PR #8. `nop` scores 0.

Generated by `scripts/kinder/gen_tasks.py` from `scripts/kinder/subset.toml`; see `docs/benchmarks/kinder.md`.
