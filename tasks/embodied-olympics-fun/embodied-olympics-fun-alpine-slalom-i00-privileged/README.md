## Task
Ski the slalom: one run down the course on your skis, both ski tips and both feet through each of the 32 gates in order, then over the finish line. (Embodied Olympics `alpine-slalom`: one Unitree G1 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
Skis glide and carve: to turn, the robot must roll its skis onto their edges and lean its body into the turn so that the snow's force passes through its centre of mass, with no walking or running policy to help; each change of turn shifts its whole body across the skis. Speed builds on the slope, and a lean that does not match the turn throws it on the snow.

## Reference solution
None. No reference controller exists for this family yet: A slalom at ICR's gentlest gradient (33 %, ICR 802.1.1) with the U16-U14 minimum vertical drop (100 m: 320 m of course, 32 open gates), a level start platform and a self-start. Positive by injection: pushed off the platform, the robot holds a slight crouch and slides straight between waypoints just outside each turning pole, re-aimed by hand at each gate; the sliding, the gate crossings and the finish are physics; deterministic. The reference skier (a carving controller after its own drop-in) passes 2 of 32 gates and falls. Negatives: nothing done (did not start), a straddle (missed gate), a stop on a flat course, a fall, a finish outside the posts..

## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in one fresh process (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success. The rules: FIS alpine slalom, one run (a course of the U16-U14 minimum vertical drop). The course is a groomed slope falling at 18.3 degrees along +x (the fall line), 40.0 m wide, centred on y = 0. Each of your feet is bound to a slalom ski. You stand on your skis on the level start platform at the top of the course, your boots 0.3 m behind its edge (the start line); the start signal is given at t = 0 and you must leave the platform by your own action (nothing holds or pushes you) within 20.0 s; your run's time starts when both feet cross the start line. There are 32 open gates: gate 1's line is 12.0 m down the slope (measured along it) from x = 0, the slope's reference line, 1 m below where the brink's curve meets the slope; each next one 9.6 m further. A gate is two flexible poles across the slope, red and blue in turn: its turning pole stands 0.8 m from the centre line, on the left (y > 0) for gate 1 and then alternately right and left, its outside pole 4.0 m further out on the same side. Its gate line is the line on the snow from the turning pole to the outside pole. A gate is passed when both ski tips (the front ends of the skis) and both feet have crossed its gate line going down the slope; a point that comes back up across a gate line must cross it again. Gates are passed in order: if any of those four points goes down past the next gate's line, or the finish line, while a gate is not passed, you missed it and the run fails. Hitting or folding a pole is allowed. The finish line is 320.0 m down the slope from x = 0, 12.0 m wide and centred on y = 0: you succeed when, after every gate, both feet cross it between its posts, within 90 s. The run fails if you fall (a part of you other than your feet and your hands touches the snow, or your pelvis is less than 0.35 m above the snow, measured along the slope's normal), if you stop after the start (your pelvis slower than 0.1 m/s for 1.5 s; ICR 614.2.3), if you do not start within 20.0 s, if a foot crosses the finish line outside its posts, or at 90 s. No oracle; human review in the pull request that adds the benchmark (PROTOCOL §4).
