## Task
MyoSuite `myoHandPenTwirlFixed-v0` (MyoHand, 39 muscles) on the same frozen instance as `myosuite-pen-twirl-i00-privileged`: Rotate the pen in the hand until its axis points along the target orientation: the cosine between the pen's axis (bottom to top) and the target's axis above 0.95, with the pen within 0.075 m of its desired position, for 100 consecutive control steps (1.0 s). Failure (the episode ends): the pen more than 0.075 m from its desired position (dropped).
A pen lies across the fingers of the upturned hand; a translucent target pen shows the goal orientation. Standard mode (eai-standard/2.2): the agent drives the body through a client in one episode, with only its own proprioception and the scene's cameras.

## Difficulty
Same instance and success rule as `myosuite-pen-twirl-i00-privileged` (first-pass rating: hard). In standard mode the agent loses resets, the simulator and the object state: it sees camera images and the limb's proprioception, acts in actuator-command chunks, and gets one episode, so it has to close the loop on what it sees and recover from its own mistakes without starting over.

## Reference solution
None. Solvability evidence: the privileged twin's positive example (Claude Code + Claude Opus 5.5 (run `claude_code-claude_opus_5_5-default`, 2026-09-28), trial `myosuite-pen-twirl-i00__UtTYzQE`, 513 rows) rounded to float32, as the client sends it, and streamed through the client: the service scores it 1 live and in both replays, in two fresh trials (docs/benchmarks/myosuite.md); human review in PR #65.

## Verification
The sim service judges the episode live and records every executed control step; the collect hook (`environment/sim/finalize.sh`) ends the service, replays the recorded trajectory in two fresh processes and writes `final.json`; `tests/grade.py` (separate sandbox, no network) passes iff the live episode succeeded, both replays succeed, and they end in the same state. No oracle; human review in PR #65. `nop`: the episode times out, reward 0.
