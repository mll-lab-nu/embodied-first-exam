# kinder-pick-place-i00-limited

Limited mode (robot as a service, `docs/LIMITED_MODE_1_1.md`) of `kinder-pick-place-i00`: the same KinDER instance
(`kinder/PickPlace3D-o1-v0`, seed 0) runs in the `sim` sidecar; the agent gets a client and the robot's own sensors -- two
cameras, joint encoders, the base's odometry -- and never the scene's state or the goal check.

Task: get the small dark-red cube on the floor in front of the robot into a square on the floor that is not marked: 30 cm on a side, its centre 1.286 m ahead of where the robot starts and 0.408 m to its left, its sides turned 2.3° clockwise from the robot's start heading (in the odometry frame). The task succeeds the moment the cube's centre is inside that square and at most 10 cm above the floor.

Generated by `scripts/kinder/gen_tasks.py` from `scripts/kinder/subset.toml`; see `docs/benchmarks/kinder.md`.
