## Task
Fly the drone through six timed laps of its figure eight, keeping close to the moving target the whole time.

## Difficulty
Infer transfer failures from video and noisy sensors across bounded adaptation rounds.

## Reference solution
Harbor 0.23.0 oracle agent on a cloud CPU host, 2026-10-18, image efe-private:control-transfer-0.2.0: the reference controller (solution/, three feedback rounds) succeeded on 10/10 held-out cases, mean engineering score 87.58; nop 0.

## Verification
Frozen development and held-out banks, data-only deployment, fresh-process state hashes, separate no-network verifier. All numeric scores and physical outcomes are operator-only until submission closes.

### Notes
Flight-data quadratic force surrogate and approximate rigid-body/actuator dynamics; not a learned aerodynamics model or flight-stack simulation. Fixed 50-degree command tilt envelope; 2.2 thrust-to-weight limit. Skydio X2 appearance only; the underlying Neural Fly surrogate is not calibrated Skydio dynamics.
