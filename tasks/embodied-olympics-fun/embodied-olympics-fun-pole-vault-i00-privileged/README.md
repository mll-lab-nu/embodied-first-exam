## Task
Vault over the bar with the pole you carry: plant its tip in the box, leave the ground hanging on it, go over the bar between the uprights and come down on the mat, with the bar still on its pegs. (Embodied Olympics `pole-vault`: one Unitree H1-2 on the Embodied Olympics simulator, judged by a referee of events, geometry and contact rules.)

## Difficulty
The bar is 0.2 m above the robot's head: the 67 kg body has to be carried over it hanging on a rigid pole that gives nothing back, by arms whose motors hold 18 to 40 N m, from a run-up that the robot's only gait makes at 0.7 m/s. Nothing built in grips, plants, swings or lets go.

## Reference solution
None. No reference controller exists for this family yet: positive by injection (the run-up, the plant and the swing-up are injected: the robot is put on the planted pole 20 degrees short of vertical; from there it really hangs, passes 4 cm over the 2.00 m bar, lets go and lands on the mat); negatives: the bar off its pegs, the ground beyond the zero line before the clearance, a hand moved up the pole, a clearance without the planted pole, round or under the bar, a hand steadying the bar.

## Verification
`/tests/grade.py` replays `/app/output/trajectory.npz` from the frozen start in two fresh processes (`rcb_rfo.py replay`:
one sim row per control step until the referee ends the attempt). Pass iff the referee's verdict is a success in both
and both end in the same state. The rules: World Athletics rules for the pole vault. You carry the pole (4.0 m long, 0.04 m thick, 2.0 kg; rigid: it does not bend) gripped in your right hand at its middle; either hand can grip it anywhere along its length, and it can be let go and gripped again. The pole touches the ground, the box, the mats, the uprights and the bar, not your body. The runway (40.0 m, 1.22 m wide) leads along +x to the box, which is sunk into the ground: its bottom is 1.00 m long and slopes down from the runway's level at x = -1.08 m to 0.20 m below it at the foot of the stop board (x = -0.10 m); the stop board leans back to the zero line x = 0 at runway level; the box is 0.60 m wide at its front and 0.15 m at the foot of the stop board (your feet do not sink into it: for them it is covered). The bar (4.5 m long, 0.03 m thick, 2.25 kg) rests with its upper side 2.0 m above the ground on two pegs (0.055 m long, 0.013 m thick, 4.3 m apart) that point towards the landing area, 0.8 m beyond the zero line; pushed towards the landing area by half a peg's length, or lifted, it falls. The landing mat (5.0 m x 5.0 m, 0.8 m high, soft) begins 0.15 m beyond the zero line (behind the box its face slopes away at 45 degrees, so the pole can lean on over it); two front pieces of the same height flank the box for 2.0 m before it. The vault: (1) plant: the pole's tip is in the box while a hand grips the pole. (2) take-off: you hang on the planted pole: for at least 0.15 s in one flight nothing of you touches anything but the pole and the bar while a hand grips the pole and its tip rests in the box. (3) clearance: in that same flight your pelvis and both feet (the ankle joints) cross the plane of the bar's nearer edge between the pegs and higher than the bar's upper side (one that comes back over the plane has not passed it), and every part of you gets beyond that plane; you may touch the bar. (4) You come down, on any part of your body, with or without the pole. You succeed when 2.0 s after your landing the bar is still on its pegs. A fall is not a failure by itself. You fail if the bar drops more than 0.05 m at any time, whether you or the pole knocked it; if, before you have cleared the bar, anything of you or the pole touches the ground or the mats beyond the zero line; if your pelvis or a foot passes the bar's plane outside the pegs or not higher than the bar; if you clear the bar without the 0.15 s on the planted pole (a jump without the pole); if, in the flight of the vault, a hand grips the pole more than 0.05 m farther from its tip than your upper hand did at lift-off (climbing); if your hands touch the bar for more than 0.1 s in all (steadying it); or after 60 s (the time allowed for a trial). No oracle; human review in the pull request that adds the benchmark (PROTOCOL §2.7).
